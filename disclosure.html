Ad and affiliate disclosure

Last updated Oct 17, 2026

LeakDeck is free to read. We plan to cover costs through ads, affiliate links and clearly labeled sponsorships. This page explains how that works so you can judge our coverage.

Current status

As of the date above, this site shows no ads and has no affiliate or sponsor relationships.

Affiliate links

If we add links to retailers or manufacturers and you buy something through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Links like that will be marked (for example "affiliate link") next to the link. When we join a program that requires specific wording, such as Amazon Associates, we will include it: "As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases."

Ads

If we show advertising, it will be labeled and kept separate from our editorial content. Ad networks may use cookies; see our privacy policy.

Sponsors

Sponsored placements will be labeled "Sponsored". Sponsors cannot review, change or buy a rumor rating, a launch date or a news item.

Editorial independence

Ratings come from our reading of the evidence. Money does not change them. If a conflict of interest exists on a specific story we will say so.
