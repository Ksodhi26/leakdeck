Acer Predator Atlas 8 (US launch)

Acer's 8-inch Windows gaming handheld with Intel's Arc G3 Extreme comes to the US on Nov 1 as a $1,299.99 Best Buy exclusive.

Acer unveiled the Atlas 8 in May; the US gets only the more powerful Intel Arc G3 Extreme version, with Arc B390 graphics (12 Xe3 GPU cores), 24GB of LPDDR5X, an 8-inch 120Hz screen, TMR sticks, dual-mode triggers and Windows 11. It is Acer's first handheld sold officially in the US and costs the same as the Xbox Ally X20. Best Buy's pre-orders, opened last week, are sold out; Acer says online orders reopen Nov 1 alongside in-store sales.
