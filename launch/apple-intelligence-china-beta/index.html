Apple Intelligence beta in China

Apple missed its October target: the first Apple Intelligence features for China start testing in the iOS developer beta on Nov 4 and reach users with iOS 27.3 in December.

Tim Cook said on Oct 15 that a beta would reach China in October; it did not. On Oct 31 (China time) Apple's China Newsroom said testing starts Nov 4 with the latest iOS developer beta, which MacRumors expects to be iOS 27.3 beta 1, likely Nov 3 in the US. The first wave is iPhone-only (iPhone 15 Pro and later, iPhone Air, iPhone Duo) with Siri and device language set to English or Simplified Chinese, and covers Live Translation, Writing Tools, Visual Intelligence, Clean Up, Genmoji, notification and mail summaries, Priority Notifications, Smart Reply and Type to Siri. Alibaba's Qwen plugs into Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence. Siri AI, Image Playground and the newer WWDC 2026 features are not included, and Mac, iPad and other devices wait on further approvals.
