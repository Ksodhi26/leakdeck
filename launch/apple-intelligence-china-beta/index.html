Apple Intelligence beta in China

The first Apple Intelligence features for China are now in testing: Apple switched them on in the iOS 27.3 developer beta on Nov 3 (US time), with a public release planned for December.

Tim Cook said on Oct 15 that a beta would reach China in October; it did not. On Oct 31 (China time) Apple's China Newsroom said testing starts Nov 4 with the latest iOS developer beta, which MacRumors expects to be iOS 27.3 beta 1, likely Nov 3 in the US. The first wave is iPhone-only (iPhone 15 Pro and later, iPhone Air, iPhone Duo) with Siri and device language set to English or Simplified Chinese, and covers Live Translation, Writing Tools, Visual Intelligence, Clean Up, Genmoji, notification and mail summaries, Priority Notifications, Smart Reply and Type to Siri. Alibaba's Qwen plugs into Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence. Siri AI, Image Playground and the newer WWDC 2026 features are not included, and Mac, iPad and other devices wait on further approvals. Update Nov 3: as of early morning Pacific time Apple had not yet posted the iOS 27.3 beta (Apple's developer releases page still tops out at iOS 27.2); MacRumors expects it later on Nov 3. Separately, Apple is testing a bug-fix iOS 27.2.1. Update Nov 4: Apple released iOS 27.3 beta 1 on Nov 3 (Nov 4 in China), and developers in China can now turn on Apple Intelligence. Per MacRumors, the beta includes Live Translation, Writing Tools (with Qwen-powered text generation), Visual Intelligence, Clean Up, Genmoji, Photos search and memory movies, Mail and notification summaries, Notes audio summaries, call transcripts, Type to Siri, and Siri handing harder questions to Qwen. Siri AI, the Siri app and the newer iOS 27 features are still excluded. A public beta and the December launch are next.
