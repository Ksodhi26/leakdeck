Apple OLED touchscreen MacBook Pro, OLED iPad mini, M6 MacBook Pro and iMac

Bloomberg says Apple will launch OLED touchscreen MacBook Pros, an OLED iPad mini, a 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro and an M6 iMac around Oct 27, with a media day in Los Angeles on Oct 28.

Apple has not announced this event. Mark Gurman reports Apple invited some media to an all-day launch experience on Oct 28 in Los Angeles, which suggests an announcement on Oct 27. Details such as which chips power the OLED models are still disputed; see the rumor tracker.
