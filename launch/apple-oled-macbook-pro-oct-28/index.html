MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display (14- and 16-inch, OLED touchscreen)

Apple's first touchscreen Mac: 14- and 16-inch MacBook Pros with a tandem OLED 'PROLED XDR' display, Dynamic Island and M5 Pro/M5 Max, announced Oct 28 and shipping Nov 6 from $2,899.

Unveiled by CEO John Ternus at Apple's 'Pro and tell' event in Los Angeles on Oct 28. The tandem OLED panel offers up to 2,000 nits peak brightness, a 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio, 10-120Hz ProMotion and P3 plus Adobe RGB color, with the touch sensor built into the panel; macOS 27 adds touch gestures and a Dynamic Island for Spotlight, Live Activities and Siri AI. It uses the existing M5 Pro and M5 Max (not M6), with up to 128GB of memory and storage now up to 16TB, a six-speaker system Apple says has 50% deeper bass, a new hinge for touch, and the glowing Apple logo returns on the lid. The 14-inch weighs about 3 pounds and the 16-inch about 4; Apple rates battery life at up to 24 hours. Base models start with 24GB of memory and 1TB of storage. Reuters says the 16-inch M5 Max starts at $4,799, and a fully specced model costs $14,549 (The Verge). Pre-orders opened Oct 28 in 30 countries and regions, with availability from Friday, Nov 6. The mini-LED MacBook Pros stay on sale. This settles the rumor in our tracker, which resolved true. As of Oct 29, AppleInsider reports 14-inch models ship Nov 6; adding nano-texture to the 16-inch moves delivery to Nov 7.
