Apple 'Pro and tell' launch: OLED touchscreen MacBook Pro, OLED iPad mini, M6 MacBook Pro and iMac (expected)

Apple has confirmed a 'Pro and tell' launch on Oct 28; Bloomberg expects OLED touchscreen MacBook Pros, an OLED iPad mini and M6 Macs, with sales from Nov 6.

Apple's Greg Joswiak posted the invite on Oct 19: a media experience in Los Angeles on Wednesday, Oct 28 at 10 a.m. PT, like the Oct 13 'Welcome home' launch, so expect press releases rather than a keynote. Apple has not named the products. Per Mark Gurman: 14- and 16-inch OLED touchscreen MacBook Pros with a Dynamic Island, 'significantly lighter' but not much thinner, using the existing M5 Pro and M5 Max (no M6 Pro/Max); an M6 14-inch MacBook Pro and M6 iMac with unchanged designs; and an OLED iPad mini. Pre-orders could open Oct 28, and Gurman says Apple Stores plan an overnight reset on Nov 5-6, pointing to a Nov 6 release.
