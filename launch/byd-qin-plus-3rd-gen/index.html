BYD Qin Plus (3rd generation, China)

BYD's redesigned third-generation Qin Plus family sedan launches in China on Oct 27, now confirmed by BYD.

On Oct 24 Lu Tian, general manager of BYD's Dynasty Network sales division, confirmed the Oct 27 launch on Weibo, after Autohome first reported the date. BYD says the Qin Plus DM-i uses its fifth-generation DM plug-in hybrid system with 2.58 L/100 km fuel use on a depleted battery (NEDC) and up to 2,290 km of combined range, offers nine exterior colors and optional 'God's Eye B' driver assistance with city and highway navigation and automated parking. The car grows to 4,840 mm long on a 2,800 mm wheelbase and adds roof-mounted LiDAR on some trims. Filings also list an EV version with a 100 kW motor. Prices are not out; the outgoing Qin Plus starts at 79,800 yuan.
