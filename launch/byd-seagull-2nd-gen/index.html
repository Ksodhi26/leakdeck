BYD Seagull (2nd generation, China)

BYD launched its bigger second-generation Seagull in China from 65,900 yuan, 4,000 yuan below the old model, with five-minute flash charging on upper trims.

Launched Oct 20 at the Zhengzhou International Circuit in five trims: Vitality (65,900 yuan), Freedom (72,900), Comfort (75,900), Flying (82,900) and Smart (99,900); trade-in buyers get 3,000 yuan off. The two cheaper trims have a 30.1kWh battery (320km CLTC) and an 85kW motor; the top three get a 39.2kWh pack (420km CLTC), a 95kW motor and BYD flash charging (10-70% in about five minutes at BYD stations), plus a year of free flash charging for private buyers. It is 425mm longer than before (4,205mm, 2,650mm wheelbase), seats five instead of four, switches to rear-motor rear-wheel drive with multi-link rear suspension, and adds a 94-litre frunk; the top trim adds roof LiDAR. Some export markets sell the Seagull as the Dolphin Mini or Dolphin Surf.
