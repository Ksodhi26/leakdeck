DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017

DeepSeek shipped an updated Flash model in its app and API and as open weights, aimed at coding and agent tasks.

DeepSeek's own benchmarks show gains over the previous Flash and some larger rivals on coding and agent tests at a much lower cost per task; independent results are not out yet. DeepSeek also opened its in-house Search and Fetch APIs to developers.
