Denza N8 EV (China)

BYD's Denza opened reservations for the all-electric five-seat N8 SUV on Oct 26; the official launch and pricing follow in November.

Denza general manager Li Hui announced the timeline on Weibo on Oct 22: reservations open Oct 26 and the car officially goes on sale in November, with no launch day, prices or delivery plans yet. MIIT filings show single-motor RWD versions (320 or 370 kW) and a tri-motor AWD version with 890 kW combined; it is 5,150 mm long on a 3,075 mm wheelbase, 50 mm shorter than the six-seat N8L. Denza says it uses BYD's second-generation Blade battery with Flash Charging, and Electrek cites up to 1,003 km of CLTC range. Reservations opened on schedule on Oct 26: a 2,000-yuan deposit earns a 5,000-yuan option credit, but Denza has still not given a pre-sale price. For reference, the six-seat N8L starts at 299,800 yuan. Earlier reports had treated Oct 26 as the launch itself.
