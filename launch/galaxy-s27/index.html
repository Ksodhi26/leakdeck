Samsung Galaxy S27 series

Samsung has not announced it, but its past Galaxy S launches point to a Jan-Feb 2027 Unpacked with sales about two weeks later.

Past Unpacked dates: Jan 17, 2024; Jan 22, 2025; Feb 25, 2026. Four models are expected: S27, S27+, S27 Pro and S27 Ultra. On Oct 19 Korean tipster Lanzuk said Samsung plans an early January 2027 launch, earlier than the S25 and S26; see the rumor tracker.
