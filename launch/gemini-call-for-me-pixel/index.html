Gemini 'Call for me' on Pixel 11 (early preview, US)

Google is rolling out Gemini 'Call for me', which phones businesses for you to check stock, get quotes or make reservations, to Pixel 11 owners in the US.

Gemini shows the number and what it will ask before dialing, introduces itself as the user's AI assistant, says the call is recorded, and gives a live transcript you can listen to or take over; afterwards it posts a summary. It can navigate phone menus and wait on hold but cannot make emergency or marketing calls or complete payments. Requirements: a Pixel 11 with a US SIM, a Google AI subscription, the public beta of the Phone by Google app, English as the device language, age 18+ and US location; it can only call US numbers and has an unspecified daily limit. Google says transcripts are stored on its servers, recordings only on the device, and neither is used for training.
