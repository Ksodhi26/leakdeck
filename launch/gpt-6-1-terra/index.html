GPT-6.1 Terra

OpenAI's new mid-tier model, aimed at fast and cheap computer use, launched Oct 26 at half the API price of GPT-6.1 Sol.

Available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and in the API as gpt-6.1-terra ($1 per million input tokens, $0.05 cached, $5 output). OpenAI says it is a big step up from GPT-5.6 Terra in visual reasoning, computer use, factual accuracy and coding (50.3% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 at max effort versus 21.5%), finishes computer-use tasks over three times faster than GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra, and beats Sol on Terminal-Bench-Science, though Sol and Astra remain the better choice for the hardest coding. OpenAI rates it at the Critical cybersecurity threshold under its Preparedness Framework. These are OpenAI's own benchmark figures.
