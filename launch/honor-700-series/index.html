Honor 700, 700 Pro and 700S (China)

Honor launched the 700, 700 Pro and 700S in China on Nov 23, all with a small rear screen, a 200MP main camera and a 9,000mAh battery.

Honor set the date in a Weibo teaser ('see you on November 13'); reservations opened Nov 6 with a 365-day replacement guarantee and a year of front and rear screen insurance. All three models have a built-in rear screen that can show a clock, animations and a selfie preview. Chips, cameras and battery sizes are still from leaks (reports point to Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 for the 700, Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 for the Pro and Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 for the 700S); prices come at the event. See the rumor tracker for the leaked specs. Update Nov 11: a device numbered FAP-AN00, most likely the 700 Pro, ran Geekbench 6 with a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, 16GB of RAM and Android 17; Honor has listed 12/256GB, 12/512GB and 16/512GB configurations for the Pro (GSMArena). Update Nov 12: Gizmochina relays a fuller spec leak ahead of the event (6.57-inch 1.5K screen, 1.72-inch rear screen, 200MP main camera and a 9,000mAh battery with 80W charging on all three; 50MP periscope and 50W wireless on the Pro), plus another Geekbench run for the Pro. Still unconfirmed until launch. Update Nov 13: the launch did not happen on Nov 13 as earlier reported. Honor CEO Li Jian set the event for Nov 23 at 2:30 PM in Wuhan; reservations stay open. A China Telecom product listing (via GSMArena) now backs most specs: 6.57-inch screen, 50MP selfie camera, 9,000mAh battery, MagicOS 11 on Android 17 and 156.0x74.7x7.96mm on all three; Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 (Pro), 8s Gen 4 (700) and 7 Gen 4 (700S); a 200MP main camera on all, plus a 50MP 3.5x periscope on the 700 and Pro. Prices come at the event. Update Nov 23: launched in Wuhan as planned. All three have a 6.57-inch 120Hz AMOLED, a 1.59-inch rear touch screen that works as a selfie viewfinder and notification panel, a 200MP main camera, a 50MP selfie camera, a 9,000mAh battery with 80W wired charging, IP69K and MagicOS 11 on Android 17. The 700 Pro has the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, a 50MP periscope and 50W wireless charging (CNY 4,599 for 12/256GB up to 16/512GB); the 700 has the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 and a 50MP periscope (CNY 3,699 to 4,599 launch prices); the 700S has the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and no periscope (CNY 3,399 to 3,699). Pre-orders are open; open sales start Nov 27 (GSMArena). Honor has not said when or if the series leaves China.
