Honor Win 2 series (China launch)

Honor launched the fan-cooled Win 2 gaming phone in China from 5,499 yuan, alongside the Win RT 2, Win Turbo 2 and its first Win gaming tablet.

Announced Oct 21 with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, a 6.89-inch 1440p 185Hz OLED screen (10,000 nits peak), a 10,100mAh battery with 100W wired and 80W wireless charging, two built-in fans (a 20,000rpm cross-flow fan and a 31,000rpm side fan), a 200MP main camera, a 12MP ultrawide, a 50MP selfie camera, IP68/IP69/IP69K ratings and MagicOS 11 on Android 17; it is 8.5mm thick and about 237g. Five memory options: 12/256GB for 5,499 yuan, 16/256GB for 5,799, 12/512GB for 6,299, 16/512GB for 6,999 and 16GB/1TB for 7,999. The Win Tablet has an 8.4-inch 240Hz OLED, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, a 10,000mAh battery and a 15,000rpm fan, from 4,199 yuan. Honor also announced the Win RT 2 and Win Turbo 2; we have not verified their final specs. No launch outside China has been announced.
