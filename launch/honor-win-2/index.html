Honor Win 2 series (China launch)

Honor's fan-cooled gaming phones launch in China on Oct 21 and go on sale the same day.

Honor has confirmed a built-in dual-fan cooling system, a 2K 185Hz display, a battery of more than 10,000mAh (10,100mAh per promo material) and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chip. Pre-orders are open, and Fone Arena reports Honor's marketing chief gave a post-subsidy price of 5,499 yuan. A Win gaming tablet debuts at the same event. Honor has not announced plans outside China. Panel maker Visionox says all three models in the series (Win 2, Win 2 RT and Win 2 Turbo) use the 2K 185Hz screen.
