Huawei Enjoy 100 series (China)

Huawei launched the Enjoy 100, 100 Pro and 100 Pro Max budget phones in China on Oct 28, from 1,399 yuan, with Kirin 8-series chips and big batteries.

Launched at Huawei's Oct 28 'Double 11' event with HarmonyOS 7. Enjoy 100: Kirin 8000S, 6,100mAh battery, 128GB (1,399 yuan) or 256GB (1,699 yuan), on sale Oct 31. Enjoy 100 Pro (from 1,799 yuan): 6.7-inch 120Hz OLED, Kirin 8020A, 50MP RYYB main camera, 32MP selfie camera, 6,100mAh with 40W charging. Enjoy 100 Pro Max (from 1,999 yuan): 6.84-inch 120Hz OLED rated up to 8,000 nits peak, 8,800mAh battery with 40W charging, Kirin 8020A on 128GB/256GB and Kirin 8020 on 512GB, 8MP selfie camera. The Pro models are on sale now. Earlier reports also named an Enjoy 100m; we have not seen its launch details.
