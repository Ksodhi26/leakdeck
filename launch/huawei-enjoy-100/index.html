Huawei Enjoy 100 series (China)

Huawei unveils four budget Enjoy 100 phones on Oct 28 in China, with Kirin 8-series chips and big batteries; pre-orders are open.

Huawei's poster names the Enjoy 100 Pro Max (Kirin 8020 on 512GB, Kirin 8020A on 256GB and 128GB), the Enjoy 100 Pro (Kirin 8020A), and the Enjoy 100 and Enjoy 100m (Kirin 8000S), with a large 'Giant Whale' battery and HarmonyOS anti-fraud features. Earlier reports point to an 8,800mAh battery, a flat 6.84-inch 1.5K screen and BeiDou satellite messaging on the Pro Max and HarmonyOS 7 across the lineup; prices come at launch.
