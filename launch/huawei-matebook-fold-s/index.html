Huawei MateBook Fold S (China)

Huawei's smaller foldable HarmonyOS PC, the 14.7-inch MateBook Fold S, opens China pre-orders on Nov 14 and adds a rear mini display.

Huawei teased the MateBook Fold S on Nov 10 as the third model in its foldable-PC line after the 18-inch MateBook Fold Ultimate and its 2026 edition. It has a 14.7-inch folding screen and a square 'Live Mini Display' on the back that users can personalize and that works while folded. Colors are Cloud Blue, Sky White and Midnight Black. Huawei has not given specs, chip or price yet.
