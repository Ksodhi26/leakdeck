Huawei Pura 90 Plus (China)

Huawei launched the Pura 90 Plus in China on Oct 28 with the Kirin 9025 and a 1-inch-type variable-aperture main camera, from 5,499 yuan; open sales start Oct 31.

Launched at Huawei's 'Double 11' event on Oct 28. Prices: 5,499 yuan (12GB/256GB), 5,999 yuan (512GB) and 6,999 yuan (1TB), with up to 300 yuan off during the Double 11 promotion (from 5,199 yuan) until Nov 30; open sales start Oct 31 at 10:08. It sits between the Pura 90 and Pura 90 Pro. Specs per Huawei and GSMArena: Kirin 9025 (Huawei claims about 20% faster than the Kirin 9020), a 6.8-inch LTPO OLED (2848x1276, 1-120Hz) with curved sides and second-gen Kunlun Glass, a 50MP 1-inch-type main camera with f/1.4-4.0 variable aperture, a 48MP 4x telephoto that doubles as macro, a 40MP ultrawide, a 13MP selfie camera, a 5,700mAh battery with 100W wired and 80W wireless charging, IP68/69, satellite connectivity and HarmonyOS 7. Colors: Sunset Purple, Dawn Gold, Snowy White and Obsidian Black.
