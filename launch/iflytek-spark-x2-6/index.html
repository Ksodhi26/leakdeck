iFlytek Spark-X2.6 (open-source)

iFlytek released and open-sourced Spark-X2.6, a 293B-parameter mixture-of-experts model trained and served on Chinese chips.

Announced at iFlytek's 1024 Global Developer Festival in Beijing, Spark-X2.6 has 293B total and 30B active parameters and a 1-million-token context window, and runs on a domestic 10,000-card inference cluster. iFlytek claims coding and agent performance comparable to leading systems, which has not been independently verified.
