iMac (M6)

The M6 iMac is on sale from Nov 6, starting at $1,499 for the 10-core model.

Announced Oct 27 by press release. The 24-inch 4.5K design and seven colors are unchanged. The M6 brings up to a 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU (two more cores each than M4) with Neural Accelerators in the GPU, a dual 16-core Neural Engine and up to 170GB/s memory bandwidth; Apple claims up to 5x faster on-device AI than M4. It adds Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6, up to 2x faster SSDs, and 2.5Gb Ethernet as an option on the 12-core model, which also gets four Thunderbolt 4 ports. Both base models have 16GB of memory and 256GB of storage. Pre-orders open Oct 28 at 10:10 a.m. PT in 35 countries and regions. As of Oct 29, AppleInsider reports every configuration still ships Nov 6. Update Nov 6: MacRumors confirms the M6 iMac is available to buy from Apple today.
