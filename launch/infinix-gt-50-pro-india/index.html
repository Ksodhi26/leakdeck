Infinix GT 50 Pro (India)

Infinix launched its liquid-cooled GT 50 Pro gaming phone in India on Oct 27 at Rs 69,999, with a gaming accessory bundle thrown in for early buyers.

The single 12GB/256GB model costs Rs 69,999 (a Rs 5,000 bank discount brings it to Rs 64,999), in Black Abyss and Red Blaze. Pre-orders are open on Flipkart and Infinix's store and sales start Oct 30. Early buyers get a bundle Infinix values at Rs 24,999 (GT NX Controller, GT NX Station dock, GT MagCharge Cooler 2.0 and a MagSafe cover). Specs: 6.78-inch 1.5K 144Hz AMOLED (up to 4,500 nits), Dimensity 8400 Ultimate with LPDDR5X and UFS 4.1, HydroFlow liquid cooling with a micro-pump, pressure-sensitive GT Triggers 2.0 and a 6,500mAh battery with 45W wired and 30W wireless charging. Digit notes the price is almost triple the GT 30 Pro's Rs 24,999 launch price and links it to the memory shortage. The phone first launched in other markets in April.
