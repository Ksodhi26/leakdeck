iOS 27.2

Apple's next iPhone update, now at release candidate, is expected around Oct 26 and brings Apple Pencil support to the iPhone Duo.

Apple released the iOS 27.2 release candidate on Oct 19, which usually means a public release about a week later; 9to5Mac expects it on or around Monday, Oct 26. Apple has not announced the date. Besides Apple Pencil (USB-C) support on the iPhone Duo, it adds features across six built-in apps and a new HomePod setting; tvOS 27.2 is expected alongside.
