iQOO Neo12 (China)

iQOO launched the fan-cooled Neo12 in China on Oct 28 with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and a 9,000mAh battery, from 4,399 yuan; sales started right away.

Prices: 4,399 yuan (12GB/256GB), 4,799 (16/256), 5,099 (12/512), 5,499 (16/512) and 6,499 (16GB/1TB); launch and platform discounts until Nov 3 plus China's national subsidy cut the entry price to 3,149 yuan where eligible. Specs: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 with iQOO's Q3 gaming chip, an active cooling fan plus vapor chamber, a 6.83-inch 2K 185Hz AMOLED (up to 2,200 nits), a 9,000mAh battery with 100W wired charging, a 50MP main camera (Sony IMX921, OIS), 8MP ultrawide, 16MP selfie camera, 3D ultrasonic fingerprint sensor and OriginOS 7 (Android 17). iQOO has not confirmed a global version.
