Leapmotor A10s (China)

Leapmotor launched a sportier-styled A10s small electric SUV in China with LiDAR and a 505km CLTC range.

Power, battery (53kWh LFP) and range match the existing A10 505 LiDAR trim; the A10s adds black paint and wheels, a red-stitched cabin and ventilated front seats for 6,000 yuan more. It uses Qualcomm's SA8295 cockpit and SA8650 driver-assist chips.
