Lenovo IdeaPad Vibe 14 (Snapdragon)

Lenovo's Snapdragon IdeaPad Vibe, pitched as a MacBook Neo rival, went on sale a month early but at $799.99 instead of the promised $699.99.

TechRadar reports it is on Lenovo's own stores in the US, UK, Canada, the Eurozone, Malaysia and Singapore, with orders shipping from Oct 28. The US base model has 8GB of soldered RAM and 256GB of storage; other markets get 512GB. It offers two USB-C, two USB-A, HDMI and microSD, and comes in seven colors. The AMD version starts at $899.
