Li Auto i6 (2026 model, China)

Li Auto launched the 2026 i6 in China on Oct 28 at an unchanged 249,800 yuan, with its own M100 chip, a 96kWh in-house battery and deliveries from early November.

The five-seat electric SUV keeps the 2025 model's 249,800-yuan price, with a 10,000-yuan launch offer until Nov 30. Li Auto's in-house Mach M100 driver-assistance chip (1,280 TOPS) replaces Nvidia's Thor-U, with a dual-M100 option that adds three more lidars. The in-house Lianshan 5C LFP battery grows to 96kWh, raising CLTC range to 780 km (RWD) and 710 km (AWD). Inside, a 29-inch 6K panoramic screen replaces the twin 15.7-inch displays. Orders are open with deliveries in as little as 1-2 weeks. A European version (Li 6) is due in Norway and the Netherlands in Q1 2027.
