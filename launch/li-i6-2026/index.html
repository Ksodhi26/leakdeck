Li Auto i6 (2026 model, China)

Li Auto launches the refreshed 2026 i6 electric SUV in China on Oct 28, with orders opening right away and deliveries in early November.

Li Auto announced the schedule on Weibo: a launch event on Oct 28, test drives and orders immediately afterward, and first deliveries in early November. The five-seat SUV switches to Li Auto's in-house 5C battery and Mach driver-assistance chip, with single-motor and dual-motor AWD versions and semi-hidden door handles. Pricing comes at launch; the current i6 starts at 249,800 yuan, and Li Auto president Liu Jie has said the new one will not be cheaper. A European version (Li 6) is due in Norway and the Netherlands in Q1 2027.
