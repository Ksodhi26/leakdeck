14-inch MacBook Pro (M6)

Apple announced the entry 14-inch MacBook Pro with the new M6 chip on Oct 27; pre-orders open Oct 28 and it ships Nov 6 from $1,999.

Announced by press release a day before Apple's Oct 28 'Pro and tell' launch. The M6 has a 12-core CPU (2 super cores, 4 performance, 6 efficiency) and a 12-core GPU, up from 10 and 10 on the M5, plus a dual 16-core Neural Engine; Apple claims up to 30% faster graphics and 25% faster AI work than M5. Memory bandwidth rises to 170GB/s, but MacRumors reports that applies only to 24GB and 32GB configurations, not the base 16GB. It adds Apple's N1 chip (Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6), drives two external 4K displays at up to 165Hz, and Apple rates battery life at up to 24 hours. The design is unchanged (mini-LED Liquid Retina XDR, space black and silver), and it still starts with 1TB of storage. Pre-orders open Oct 28 in 35 countries and regions.
