14-inch MacBook Pro (M6)

The entry 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 is on sale from Nov 6, starting at $1,999.

Announced by press release a day before Apple's Oct 28 'Pro and tell' launch. The M6 has a 12-core CPU (2 super cores, 4 performance, 6 efficiency) and a 12-core GPU, up from 10 and 10 on the M5, plus a dual 16-core Neural Engine; Apple claims up to 30% faster graphics and 25% faster AI work than M5. Memory bandwidth rises to 170GB/s, but MacRumors reports that applies only to 24GB and 32GB configurations, not the base 16GB. It adds Apple's N1 chip (Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6), drives two external 4K displays at up to 165Hz, and Apple rates battery life at up to 24 hours. The design is unchanged (mini-LED Liquid Retina XDR, space black and silver), and it still starts with 1TB of storage. Pre-orders open Oct 28 in 35 countries and regions. As of Oct 29, most configurations still ship Nov 6, but AppleInsider reports nano-texture orders slipping (7-10 business days in silver, 3-4 weeks in space black). Update Nov 6: MacRumors confirms the M6 14-inch MacBook Pro is available to buy from Apple today alongside the rest of the Nov 6 lineup.
