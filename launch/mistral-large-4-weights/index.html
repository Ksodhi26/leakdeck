Mistral Large 4 open weights

Mistral released the open weights for Large 4 ('Le Chonk'), its 1-trillion-parameter model, on Oct 29, two days ahead of its ETA.

Previewed Oct 6 as a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model (49B active parameters per token, 52B counting embeddings and output layers), trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's European data centers. Mistral announced the weights release on Oct 29. The Next Web reports three more weeks of reinforcement learning since the preview (Terminal-Bench 4.0 up from 28% to 41%), new low and medium reasoning modes, and a quantized version that runs on four NVIDIA B200 or B300 GPUs. It is on Hugging Face, Mistral's API and for some Vibe users. Smaller distilled versions, including one for laptops, are planned with no dates. Rechecked Oct 30: the Hugging Face model page is live with downloadable weights, an NVFP4 quantized checkpoint and a 1M-token context.
