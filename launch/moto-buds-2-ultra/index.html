Moto Buds 2 Ultra

Motorola's Bose-tuned Moto Buds 2 Ultra earbuds were announced Nov 5 for €199, arriving in the coming weeks.

11mm drivers with Bose SoundDesign tuning and Bose QuietControl ANC, Transparency Mode, Dolby Atmos with head tracking, AI live translation on compatible Moto phones, IP57, and up to 8 hours per charge (37 hours with the case). Same regional rollout as the Watch 2 Fit; some markets call them Moto Buds Ultra 27.
