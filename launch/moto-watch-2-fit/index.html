Moto Watch 2 Fit

Motorola's Polar-powered Moto Watch 2 Fit fitness watch was announced Nov 5 and arrives in the coming weeks from €119.

Announced alongside the Signature 27 ecosystem push. 1.85-inch always-on OLED (1,500 nits, 60Hz), Gorilla Glass 3, IP68 and 5ATM, up to 12 days of battery, and Polar features such as Running Index, Nightly Recharge and Daily Activity Score. Colors: Stratified Sea, Pigeon and Ballerina. Selling in Europe, Middle East, Latin America, North America and Asia-Pacific; some markets use the name Moto Watch Fit 27. An Ask Anything / 'Hey Qira' AI coach is promised later.
