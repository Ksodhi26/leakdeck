Motorola Signature 27 (US)

Motorola's Signature 27 is official at $1,599 in the US, with pre-orders open now and sales from Nov 19; Europe gets it Nov 16.

Unveiled at Qualcomm's Snapdragon Summit in September with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 (Motorola's page calls it 'Extreme'). Motorola's US landing page says 'coming this November', and an India page now says the same. European retailer listings point to about EUR 1,595 (see the rumor tracker); Motorola has not given US pricing. Update Oct 30: Motorola said on X that it will show the phone on Nov 5; it has not said whether that is also the US on-sale date. Update Oct 31: a Signature 27 on Android 17 with 12GB of RAM appeared on Geekbench (3,313 single-core, 11,088 multi-core); Motorola has confirmed a 50MP Sony LYT 910 main camera, a 200MP periscope, a larger vapor chamber and seven years of OS updates, but still no US price or on-sale date. Update Nov 2: Motorola confirmed more specs through a sponsored preview with YouTuber Danny Winget, including a tandem OLED screen it rates at up to 12,000 nits peak, a 50MP Sony LYTIA main sensor with LOFIC, aluminum and ceramic materials and two Pantone colors. Price and US on-sale date are still expected on Nov 5. Update Nov 5: Motorola fully announced the phone. US: one 12GB/512GB model at $1,599, pre-orders open on Motorola.com, Best Buy and Amazon, on sale Nov 19, unlocked, in Coal Smoke and Capulet Olive, with a free Moto Watch Ultra as a pre-order offer; no Canada launch. Europe, Middle East and Africa: Nov 16, at a recommended EUR 1,899, though GSMArena says some retailers and partners will sell it for EUR 1,399. Specs: Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, 6.8-inch tandem OLED (12,000 nits peak), 7,000mAh silicon-carbon battery with 90W wired and 50W wireless charging, 50MP main, 50MP ultrawide and 200MP 3x periscope cameras plus a dedicated event-vision 'action' sensor, IP68/IP69, 227 g, Android 17 with seven years of updates.
