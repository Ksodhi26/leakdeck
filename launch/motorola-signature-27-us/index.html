Motorola Signature 27 (US)

Motorola's US site says its Snapdragon flagship, the Signature 27, is coming in November; the exact date and price are not out.

Unveiled at Qualcomm's Snapdragon Summit in September with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 (Motorola's page calls it 'Extreme'). Motorola's US landing page says 'coming this November', and an India page now says the same. European retailer listings point to about EUR 1,595 (see the rumor tracker); Motorola has not given US pricing.
