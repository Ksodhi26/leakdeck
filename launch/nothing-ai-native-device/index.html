Nothing's first AI-native device (screenless)

Nothing will announce its first AI-native, screenless device on Dec 8; teasers point to a voice-note recorder.

Pei announced the December timing in an essay posted on X on Nov 4, with a teaser image showing only rounded corners and curved edges. Nothing has not named the product or given its form factor, price or exact date, and pitches it as a productivity tool first. Earlier reports said Nothing is working on AI wearables, including smart glasses, but that is not confirmed as this device. Update Nov 19: a photo posted by DJ Sebastian Ingrosso shows a small, screenless palm-sized device that Android Authority says its sources confirm is Nothing's AI product. It has two buttons (one for recording, one labeled 'TALK') and red and green LEDs, which hints at voice notes or meeting capture, but Nothing has not said what it does. Update Nov 20: Nothing posted on X 'Be present. It's noted. 8 December.' with a close-up of a brushed metal casing and a round button (Android Authority). The date is official; the product name, what it does, price and whether Dec 8 is a full launch or only a reveal are not.
