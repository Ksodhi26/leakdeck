NVIDIA RTX Spark Windows laptops (Surface Laptop Ultra and more)

Laptops built on NVIDIA's RTX Spark chip began shipping Oct 16 from Asus, Microsoft, Lenovo, HP and Dell.

RTX Spark pairs a Grace CPU with a Blackwell GPU and up to 128GB of unified memory. Tom's Hardware lists prices from $2,599 (Asus ProArt P14) to about $7,000 for a 128GB, 2TB configuration. Small desktops are due later.
