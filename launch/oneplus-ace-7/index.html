OnePlus Ace 7 and Ace 7 Pro (China)

OnePlus launched the fan-cooled Ace 7 (from 4,399 yuan) and Ace 7 Pro (from 4,799 yuan) in China on Oct 29, both with 9,000mAh batteries and 200MP main cameras.

Launched and on sale in China on Oct 29. The Ace 7 uses a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 'V series' chip and the Ace 7 Pro the full Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5. Both have a 6.78-inch 1.5K LTPO screen (165Hz system-wide, 185Hz in games), a built-in dual-airflow fan, a 9,000mAh battery with 100W charging and bypass charging, a 200MP main camera, a 16MP selfie camera, IP66/68/69/69K ratings and ColorOS 17. The Pro adds a 50MP 3.5x periscope telephoto; the standard model gets an 8MP ultrawide instead. Ace 7 prices: 4,399 yuan (12GB/256GB) to 5,699 yuan (16GB/512GB). Ace 7 Pro: 4,799 yuan (12GB/256GB) to 7,299 yuan (16GB/1TB). China's national subsidy lowers the starting price to 2,761.65 yuan for the Ace 7 and 3,149 yuan for the Pro.
