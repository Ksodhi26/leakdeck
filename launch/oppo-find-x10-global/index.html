OPPO Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max (global launch)

OPPO launched the Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max globally on Oct 21 at EUR 1,499 and EUR 1,799, with European pre-orders open and deliveries from Nov 2.

Finnish pricing published at launch: Find X10 12GB/512GB EUR 1,499 and Find X10 Pro Max 12GB/512GB EUR 1,799, about EUR 500 more than last year's Find X9 equivalents, with pre-order discounts of at least EUR 100 and EUR 200 and deliveries starting Nov 2; a Hasselblad Teleconverter Kit costs EUR 499. The European models have a 7,150mAh battery (8,000mAh in China), 80W wired and 50W wireless charging, IP66/IP68/IP69 ratings and ColorOS 17 on Android 17. The Pro Max has three 200MP rear cameras (main, ultrawide and 3x telephoto) and the 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro; the Find X10 pairs 200MP main and telephoto cameras with a 50MP ultrawide. In the Benelux, OPPO's store opened balance payments at 17:00 CEST on Oct 21; Singapore pre-orders open Oct 22 with deliveries from Oct 26; India gets its launch Oct 22. There is no US launch.
