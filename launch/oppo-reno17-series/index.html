Oppo Reno17 series, Pad Air 6 and Watch 5 (China)

Oppo launches the Reno17 and Reno17 Pro in China on Nov 16, alongside the Pad Air 6 tablet and Watch 5.

Announced on Oppo's Weibo on Oct 30 without a date, prices or specs. Leaks point to a Reno17 and Reno17 Pro in China on ColorOS 17 (Android 17); Digital Chat Station tips the Pro with a 6.32-inch 1.5K 120Hz OLED, Dimensity 8550 Super, a 200MP main camera, 50MP periscope and about 7,000mAh, but those specs are unconfirmed. Update Nov 3: Digital Chat Station now tips the Reno17 with a 6.5-inch screen, Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and an 8,000mAh-class battery, and the Reno17 Pro with a 6.3-inch screen, Dimensity 8550 and a 7,200mAh-class battery; both reportedly get a 50MP periscope, in 8GB/256GB to 12GB/512GB versions. Still a leak; Oppo has confirmed only a November launch. Update Nov 9: Oppo confirmed on Weibo that the Reno17 series, Watch 5 and Pad Air 6 launch in China on Nov 16, revealed the phones' design and four colors (Adventure Cat, Cocoa Brown, First Snow White, Ice Moon White) and opened pre-orders. Oppo also published the Pad Air 6 spec sheet ahead of launch: a 12.1-inch 2.8K 120Hz LCD (matte option), Dimensity 7360 Super, 10,050mAh battery, 8MP front and rear cameras, up to 12GB/256GB, Wi-Fi and LTE versions, ColorOS 17; pricing comes at launch. The Watch 5 is 9.2mm thick, 25g and rated for up to 7 days. Phone specs are still unannounced. Update Nov 11: Oppo's official teaser confirms the standard Reno17 has four 50MP cameras (main, periscope telephoto and ultra-wide on the back, plus an ultra-wide selfie camera), dropping the 200MP main sensor of the Reno16; the Reno17 Pro's cameras are not yet confirmed. The event starts at 7 PM China time (Gizmochina).
