Oppo Reno17 series, Pad Air 6 and Watch 5 (China)

Oppo launched the Reno17 and a smaller Reno17 Pro in China on Nov 16, plus the Pad Air 6 tablet and Watch 5, with sales starting Nov 20.

Announced on Oppo's Weibo on Oct 30 without a date, prices or specs. Leaks point to a Reno17 and Reno17 Pro in China on ColorOS 17 (Android 17); Digital Chat Station tips the Pro with a 6.32-inch 1.5K 120Hz OLED, Dimensity 8550 Super, a 200MP main camera, 50MP periscope and about 7,000mAh, but those specs are unconfirmed. Update Nov 3: Digital Chat Station now tips the Reno17 with a 6.5-inch screen, Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and an 8,000mAh-class battery, and the Reno17 Pro with a 6.3-inch screen, Dimensity 8550 and a 7,200mAh-class battery; both reportedly get a 50MP periscope, in 8GB/256GB to 12GB/512GB versions. Still a leak; Oppo has confirmed only a November launch. Update Nov 9: Oppo confirmed on Weibo that the Reno17 series, Watch 5 and Pad Air 6 launch in China on Nov 16, revealed the phones' design and four colors (Adventure Cat, Cocoa Brown, First Snow White, Ice Moon White) and opened pre-orders. Oppo also published the Pad Air 6 spec sheet ahead of launch: a 12.1-inch 2.8K 120Hz LCD (matte option), Dimensity 7360 Super, 10,050mAh battery, 8MP front and rear cameras, up to 12GB/256GB, Wi-Fi and LTE versions, ColorOS 17; pricing comes at launch. The Watch 5 is 9.2mm thick, 25g and rated for up to 7 days. Phone specs are still unannounced. Update Nov 11: Oppo's official teaser confirms the standard Reno17 has four 50MP cameras (main, periscope telephoto and ultra-wide on the back, plus an ultra-wide selfie camera), dropping the 200MP main sensor of the Reno16; the Reno17 Pro's cameras are not yet confirmed. The event starts at 7 PM China time (Gizmochina). Update Nov 13: Oppo confirmed a 200MP main camera for the Reno17 Pro, the Dimensity 8550 Super chip for the Pro and the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 for the Reno17, which gets four 50MP cameras; both run ColorOS 17 on Android 17. Update Nov 14: Digital Chat Station leaked near-complete specs (via Gizmochina): the Reno17 with a 6.56-inch 1.5K flat screen, Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, four 50MP cameras including a 3.5x periscope, and an 8,000mAh battery with 80W charging; the Reno17 Pro with a 6.32-inch 1.5K flat screen, Dimensity 8550 Super, a 200MP main camera plus 50MP ultrawide and 50MP 3.5x periscope, and a 7,200mAh battery with 80W charging. Battery, charging and screen figures are still leaks until the Nov 16 event (7 PM China time); prices are unannounced. Update Nov 16: launched as planned. The Reno17 Pro has a 6.32-inch 1.5K 120Hz flat OLED (1,800 nits), the Dimensity 8550 Super, a 200MP main camera with OIS, a 50MP ultrawide, a 50MP 3.5x periscope and a 50MP selfie camera, and a 7,250mAh battery with 80W charging; it costs 4,199 yuan (8GB/256GB), 4,599 yuan (12/256GB) and 4,999 yuan (12/512GB). The Reno17 has a 6.56-inch 120Hz screen, the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, four 50MP cameras including a 3.5x periscope, and an 8,000mAh battery with 80W charging, Oppo's largest in a Reno; it costs 3,599, 3,799, 3,999 and 4,899 yuan (8/256GB up to 12GB/1TB). Both are IP69K rated and run ColorOS 17. China's national subsidy cuts about 500 yuan. GSMArena notes the specs closely match the international Reno16 series. The Pad Air 6 lists at 3,099 yuan (2,599 yuan launch price) and the Watch 5 starts at 899 yuan; everything goes on sale Nov 20. The leaked specs held up, apart from the Pro's battery coming in at 7,250mAh rather than 7,200mAh.
