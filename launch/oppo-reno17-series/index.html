Oppo Reno17 series, Pad Air 6 and Watch 5 (China)

Oppo opened blind reservations in China for the Reno17 phones and confirmed a November launch alongside the Pad Air 6 tablet and Watch 5.

Announced on Oppo's Weibo on Oct 30 without a date, prices or specs. Leaks point to a Reno17 and Reno17 Pro in China on ColorOS 17 (Android 17); Digital Chat Station tips the Pro with a 6.32-inch 1.5K 120Hz OLED, Dimensity 8550 Super, a 200MP main camera, 50MP periscope and about 7,000mAh, but those specs are unconfirmed. Update Nov 3: Digital Chat Station now tips the Reno17 with a 6.5-inch screen, Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and an 8,000mAh-class battery, and the Reno17 Pro with a 6.3-inch screen, Dimensity 8550 and a 7,200mAh-class battery; both reportedly get a 50MP periscope, in 8GB/256GB to 12GB/512GB versions. Still a leak; Oppo has confirmed only a November launch.
