RedMagic 12 Pro (global)

RedMagic's liquid-cooled gaming phone with RGB lighting, a 185Hz screen and an 8,400mAh battery goes on sale worldwide Nov 18.

Announced globally on Nov 3 as the international version of China's RedMagic 12 Pro+. It has the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, a 6.85-inch 1.5K 185Hz OLED with an under-display 16MP selfie camera, RGB-lit dual-loop liquid cooling plus a fan, an 8,400mAh battery, 50MP main and ultrawide cameras, and IP68/IP69 ratings. The global model charges at 80W (120W in China). Prices are about EUR 200 higher than last year's RedMagic 11 Pro, and the top model drops from 24GB to 16GB of RAM. Early-bird vouchers sell Nov 12-17, voucher holders can order Nov 17, and open sales start Nov 18 in North America, most of the EU, the UK and parts of Asia Pacific, the Middle East and Latin America.
