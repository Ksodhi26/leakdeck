Redmi K100 (China)

Redmi launched the standard K100 in China on Oct 27 with a 10,000mAh battery and Snapdragon 8 Elite, from 3,499 yuan; sales start Oct 31.

List prices are 3,499 yuan (12GB/256GB), 4,099 yuan (12GB/512GB) and 4,499 yuan (16GB/512GB); Double 11 offers cut each by 500-600 yuan until Nov 11 (from 2,999 yuan), and China's national subsidy lowers it further in eligible regions. Pre-sales opened at launch and open sales start Oct 31 at 10 a.m. It pairs the Snapdragon 8 Elite with Redmi's D2 display chip (a first for a standard K model), a 10,000mAh silicon-carbon battery with 100W wired charging, a flat 6.83-inch 165Hz AMOLED screen, a 50MP main camera, IP68 and HyperOS 4. Earlier reports had suggested about 3,500 yuan, which matched the list price.
