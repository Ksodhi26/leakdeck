Ring Rover Cam

Ring's $999.99 Rover Cam, a security robot built on Amazon's Astro, arrives Dec 16 for small and medium businesses.

Announced Nov 10 with the Ring Drone Cam. The Rover Cam looks almost identical to Amazon Astro (whose $2,350 business version was discontinued in 2024) and targets small and medium businesses. It has a 4K camera on a periscope that extends up to 4 feet, a siren, a light tower, a speaker and microphone, scheduled or event-triggered patrols, and person-motion detection while docked. Pre-orders are open and The Verge says shipping starts Dec 16, the same day Engadget says the updated Alexa Home Map software arrives. A Ring Protect plan is needed for video storage.
