Samsung Galaxy Buds On (US)

Samsung's clip-on Galaxy Buds On are on sale in the US from Nov 6 for $229.99, in black only.

Announced Oct 28, a day after Korean sales began. Samsung lists a TwinBoost speaker with sound-leakage reduction, automatic volume (a Labs feature), three mics plus a voice pickup sensor for calls, head gestures, Gemini Live and Bixby voice access, IP54 earbuds weighing 6.65g each, Bluetooth 6.2 and up to 9.5 hours of playback (38 hours with the case). Samsung's US product page and Android Authority give Nov 6 as the US on-sale date, in one black color and one size; they use Qualcomm's Snapdragon S7 Gen 1 Sound Platform. Update Nov 6: Android Authority and SamMobile report US sales are live at $229.99 from Samsung.com, Experience Stores and retailers. Update Nov 7: SamMobile's sources say the European launch comes in the third week of January 2027; Samsung itself has only said 'early 2027'.
