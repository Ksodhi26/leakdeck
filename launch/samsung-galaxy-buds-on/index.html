Samsung Galaxy Buds On (South Korea)

Samsung's first clip-style open-ear Galaxy Buds go on sale in South Korea on Oct 27, with other markets later.

Samsung says the Galaxy Buds On rest on the ear without blocking the canal, with Twin Boost speakers, sound-leakage reduction, automatic volume adjustment, three mics plus a bone-conduction sensor for calls, head-gesture control and up to 9.5 hours of playback. Korea gets one black model. Samsung has not set dates for other markets; European retail listings reported by Android Authority hint at the first half of December, which is unconfirmed.
