Samsung Galaxy F70 5G (India)

Samsung launched the Galaxy F70 5G in India on Oct 18; first sales start Oct 20 at noon IST on Flipkart and Samsung's site.

It has a 6.7-inch 90Hz Full HD+ Super AMOLED screen with Gorilla Glass Victus, the Exynos 1330, a 50MP main camera with OIS, a 5,000mAh battery with 25W charging (charger sold separately) and a 7.5mm body, in Bold Black and Vivid Violet. Bank offers cut Rs 2,000 off at launch. Reports disagree on whether it ships with Android 15 or a newer One UI build.
