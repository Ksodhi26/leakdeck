Samsung Intelligent Eyewear (Android XR glasses)

Samsung's audio-first Android XR Intelligent Eyewear opens US pre-orders on Nov 12 through Warby Parker, with pricing still TBA.

Developed with Google; frames from Warby Parker and Gentle Monster. Android Authority and SamMobile say pre-orders open Nov 12, when the full collection, lens options and pricing are due. Shipping has been tipped for mid-December. Audio-first design first; display-integrated Samsung models come later. Privacy features include a recording LED that blocks capture if covered. Update Nov 6: Gentle Monster posted on X that its first Intelligent Eyewear collection 'launches this December', alongside a Joaquin Phoenix short film due mid-November. That is the first official December release window; Warby Parker has not yet given its ship date. Update Nov 10: SamMobile found pairing support and frame images for Gentle Monster and Warby Parker models (codename Jinju, model numbers SM-0200J to SM-0205J and SM-0200P to SM-0204P) in the latest Galaxy Wearable app, another sign the Nov 12 pre-orders are on track.
