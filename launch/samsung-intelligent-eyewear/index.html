Samsung Intelligent Eyewear (Android XR glasses)

Samsung and Google's audio-first Android XR Intelligent Eyewear is on pre-order in the US and Korea from $395 and ships Dec 10.

Developed with Google; frames from Warby Parker and Gentle Monster. Android Authority and SamMobile say pre-orders open Nov 12, when the full collection, lens options and pricing are due. Shipping has been tipped for mid-December. Audio-first design first; display-integrated Samsung models come later. Privacy features include a recording LED that blocks capture if covered. Update Nov 6: Gentle Monster posted on X that its first Intelligent Eyewear collection 'launches this December', alongside a Joaquin Phoenix short film due mid-November. That is the first official December release window; Warby Parker has not yet given its ship date. Update Nov 10: SamMobile found pairing support and frame images for Gentle Monster and Warby Parker models (codename Jinju, model numbers SM-0200J to SM-0205J and SM-0200P to SM-0204P) in the latest Galaxy Wearable app, another sign the Nov 12 pre-orders are on track. Update Nov 12: pre-orders opened in the US and South Korea through Warby Parker, Gentle Monster and select Samsung stores, with retail availability on Dec 10. Prices run $395-$489 (Warby Parker from $395 in 14 variants; Gentle Monster from $449 in 16). Specs: Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1, 2GB RAM, 64GB storage, 12MP ultra-wide camera (3K 30fps video), four mics, open-ear speakers, 9 hours per charge, IPX4, about 41g. Works with Galaxy phones on One UI 9.0+, Pixel 7 and newer, and iPhone 13 and newer (9to5Google, SamMobile).
