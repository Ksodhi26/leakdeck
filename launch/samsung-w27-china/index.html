Samsung W-series foldable (expected W27, China)

Samsung China will launch a new gold-framed book-style foldable on Oct 29, widely expected to be the China-only W27.

Samsung confirmed the Oct 29 date on Weibo with an image of a gold-framed book-style foldable, and its linked page references 'W2026' and the W series. Samsung has not named the phone. Tipster Ice Universe says it is the W27 with the same specs as the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy, 5,000mAh, 45W, 200MP main camera), plus the usual W-series extras. W models are normally sold only in China.
