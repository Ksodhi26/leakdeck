Samsung W27 (China)

Samsung and China Telecom launched the China-only W27, a luxury Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, on Oct 29 from 18,999 yuan; sales start Nov 13.

Launched Oct 29 at Samsung and China Telecom's annual W-series ('Heart of the World') event. IT Home says it is built on the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy, an 8-inch inner and 6.5-inch outer screen, a triple rear camera with a 200MP main sensor and a 5,000mAh battery, in a two-tone design with a gold-finished frame, in blue and red. Ifeng lists 215 g and 4.1 mm unfolded. Prices are 18,999 yuan for 16GB/512GB and 21,999 yuan for 16GB/1TB. Registration opened Oct 29 and sales start on all channels on Nov 13. It is sold only in China.
