Smart #2 (China launch)

Smart's two-seat ForTwo successor went on sale in China on Nov 5 in a single Premium trim for 102,900 yuan, or 99,900 with launch incentives.

Yiche reported the Nov 5 date citing official information, and Smart's own China media site and IT Home carry the same date. The #2 debuted at the Paris Motor Show on Oct 12, when Smart put 999 Couture Edition cars on sale in China at 112,999 yuan. It uses a 35.8 kWh CATL LFP battery (up to 410 km CLTC), a 60 kW motor and 10-80% charging in 20 minutes, rides on the Electric Compact Architecture designed for tiny EVs, and is built by Geely in China for China, Europe and other markets. Prices for the regular versions come at launch. Update Nov 2: Smart says the 999-unit European Couture Edition (from EUR 25,690) sold out within two weeks of early orders opening on Oct 13; the regular Pure, Pure+ and Premium versions start at EUR 19,990 in Germany, France and Italy, with European deliveries planned for mid Q2 2027 (WLTP range up to 290 km). Launch, Nov 5: the regular car comes in one Premium trim at a list price of 102,900 yuan, with a 3,000 yuan launch incentive bringing it to 99,900 yuan, plus trade-in and repeat-buyer subsidies. The 999-unit China Couture Edition has sold out and delivers this year.
