Tesla Cybercab sales to customers

Tesla said on its Q3 earnings call that it will start selling the steering-wheel-free Cybercab to customers by the end of 2026.

Tesla engineering VP Lars Moravy said current Cybercab production goes to Tesla's own robotaxi fleet, with customer deliveries starting small later. Musk said most Cybercabs will still be Tesla-owned and sold as transport-as-a-service. NHTSA opened an investigation in September into whether the Cybercab meets federal safety standards, which could affect the plan. On the same call CFO Vaibhav Taneja said Cybercab robotaxi service expands from Austin to Dallas next month and to more cities by year-end. Treat Tesla timelines with caution.
