Tesla unsupervised FSD for owner cars (Robotaxi zones only)

Tesla now targets Q2 2027 for unsupervised FSD in customer cars, but only by adding them to its Robotaxi fleet inside Robotaxi zones.

On the Q3 2026 earnings call, Tesla AI chief Ashok Elluswamy gave Q2 2027 and described the first step as owners adding their cars to Tesla's managed Robotaxi network, so cars would only drive unsupervised where Robotaxi already operates. Tesla has missed several earlier unsupervised-FSD targets.
