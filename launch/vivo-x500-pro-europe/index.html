vivo X500 Pro and X500 Pro Max (Europe launch)

vivo launched the X500 Pro and X500 Pro Max in Europe on Oct 27, from €1,399 and €1,599, with launch discounts and free extras until Nov 30.

Both run MediaTek's 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro. The Pro Max has a 200MP ZEISS APO telephoto camera and supports a 400mm-equivalent clip-on teleconverter; the smaller Pro has a 6.36-inch screen. The standard X500 is not part of the European launch. It launched at an event in Tenerife on Oct 27. List prices: X500 Pro 12GB/256GB €1,399 and 12GB/512GB €1,599; X500 Pro Max 12GB/256GB €1,599, 12GB/512GB €1,799 and 12GB/1TB €2,199 (1TB only through vivo's own store). European models all have 12GB of RAM. Early-bird offers (lower launch prices in some countries, a registration voucher, the camera grip with the Pro Max or vivo Headphones Studio with the Pro) run until Nov 30; exact deals vary by country.
