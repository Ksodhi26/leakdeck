vivo X500 Pro and X500 Pro Max (Europe launch)

vivo's ZEISS camera flagships, already out in China, launch in Europe at an event on Oct 27.

Both run MediaTek's 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro. The Pro Max has a 200MP ZEISS APO telephoto camera and supports a 400mm-equivalent clip-on teleconverter; the smaller Pro has a 6.36-inch screen. vivo says prices, configurations and regional availability come at the event. The standard X500 is not part of the European launch.
