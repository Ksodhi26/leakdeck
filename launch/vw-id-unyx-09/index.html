Volkswagen ID. UNYX 09 (China)

VW's second Xpeng co-developed EV, a large fastback sedan with 5C fast charging and up to 755 km CLTC range, launched in China.

Volkswagen Anhui lists three trims at 179,900, 219,900 and 249,900 yuan; the entry price is 20,000 yuan below the pre-sale price from Sept 24. A limited Double 11 price of 169,900 yuan applies to 500 black Ultra cars. The car is 5,081 mm long on a 3,030 mm wheelbase, and follows the ID. UNYX 08 SUV as the second model built with Xpeng.
