Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max (global launch)

Xiaomi says its 18 Pro series, launched in China in September, goes global in November, the first time its Pro models leave China.

The Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max launched in China on Sept 23 with dual 200MP Leica cameras, rear displays and Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chips (Extreme Gen 6 on the Pro Max). On Nov 2 Xiaomi said on X that the series is 'going global this November', without a date or prices. Last year only the standard Xiaomi 17 left China. European retailer listings point to smaller batteries and prices from about EUR 1,399; see the rumor tracker. Reports suggest India may get only the 18 Pro. Update Nov 3: Xiaomi president Lu Weibing repeated the November timing on Weibo; still no date, markets or prices.
