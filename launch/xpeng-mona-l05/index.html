Xpeng Mona L05 (China)

Xpeng launched the Mona L05 SUV in China on Oct 29 with a limited-time starting price of 139,800 yuan, below its pre-sale price.

Six versions at launch: four battery-electric (up to 635 km CLTC) and two extended-range (310 km on battery, 1,310 km combined). Official guide prices run 144,800-176,800 yuan; buyers who pay a 5,000 yuan deposit by Dec 15 get 5,000 yuan off, for 139,800-171,800 yuan. Launch added a new entry 560 Plus BEV version, and the five pre-sale versions are each 15,000 yuan cheaper than their Oct 15 pre-sale prices. Max trims get one Xpeng Turing chip (750 TOPS) and the second-gen VLA Lite driver-assist model; Ultra SE trims get two chips (1,500 TOPS) and the full VLA model. It is 4,870 mm long on a 2,940 mm wheelbase, slotting above the Mona L03. Global shipments started Oct 26 for 65 countries and regions; overseas delivery dates are not set.
