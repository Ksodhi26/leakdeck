Xpeng Mona L05 (China)

Xpeng's mid-size Mona L05 SUV, in pre-sale since Oct 15 from 169,800 yuan, officially launches in China on Oct 29.

Five versions: three battery-electric (58.2 or 71.1 kWh LFP, 535-635 km CLTC) and two extended-range (1.5-liter engine with a 37.2 kWh battery, 310 km on battery and 1,310 km combined), all with a 183 kW rear motor and adaptive dampers. Max trims get one Xpeng Turing chip (750 TOPS), Ultra SE trims two (1,500 TOPS). Pre-sale prices run 169,800-191,800 yuan; final prices come at launch. It is 4,870 mm long on a 2,940 mm wheelbase, slotting above the Mona L03.
