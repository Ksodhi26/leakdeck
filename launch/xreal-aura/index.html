XREAL Aura (Android XR glasses)

The first Android XR glasses, XREAL's tethered Aura, went on sale Oct 28 for $1,099 and start shipping in mid-November.

The 95-gram transparent glasses connect by cable to a compute puck running Android XR, with a 70-degree field of view, multiple virtual screens and Gemini. Orders are open in the US, Canada and South Korea; Japan sales start Nov 2 and wider availability comes next year. The launch prices of $1,099 and $1,299 run through the end of 2026 (regular prices $1,279 and $1,499), and XREAL says earlier pre-order buyers get the difference refunded.
