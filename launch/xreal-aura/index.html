XREAL Aura (Android XR glasses)

The first Android XR glasses, XREAL's tethered Aura, went on sale Oct 28 for $1,099; XREAL says Founder Edition orders start shipping Nov 17.

The 95-gram transparent glasses connect by cable to a compute puck running Android XR, with a 70-degree field of view, multiple virtual screens and Gemini. Orders are open in the US, Canada and South Korea; Japan sales start Nov 2 and wider availability comes next year. The launch prices of $1,099 and $1,299 run through the end of 2026 (regular prices $1,279 and $1,499), and XREAL says earlier pre-order buyers get the difference refunded. Update Nov 15: XREAL said on Threads that Founder Edition orders begin shipping on Nov 17, with first deliveries in the following days; other orders ship in batches by priority tier and payment date. XREAL Japan lists the general Japanese release for Nov 27 at 14:00, with shipping from late November. Update Nov 17: 9to5Google published an initial review on the day XREAL said Founder Edition shipments begin; we will mark it Released once deliveries are confirmed. Update Nov 18: Tom's Hardware has also published a review, but we have not yet seen confirmation of customer deliveries, so it stays Confirmed for now. Update Nov 19: Engadget and WIRED have published reviews too, but we still have not seen confirmed customer deliveries, so it stays Confirmed. Update Nov 20: TechRadar has published another review, but we still have no confirmed customer deliveries, so it stays Confirmed.
