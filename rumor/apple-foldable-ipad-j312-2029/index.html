Apple's 19-20-inch foldable iPad (J312) slips to around 2029, at $3,000-$5,000

The claim

Apple is still developing Project J312, a foldable iPad with a roughly 19-20-inch screen and no outer display, which could cost $3,000-$5,000. Its target has slipped from 2027 to 2028 to around 2029, and its operating system (macOS, iPadOS or something new) is undecided.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Bloomberg's Mark Gurman on Power On (MacRumors, AppleInsider), consistent with his earlier reports of a 2029 timeframe. Gurman himself says cost, display yields and bulk could kill the project. In the same episode he said Apple runs macOS on some iPads internally for testing but has no plan to ship macOS on iPad (GSMArena, 9to5Mac). Long-range and uncertain: Rumor.
