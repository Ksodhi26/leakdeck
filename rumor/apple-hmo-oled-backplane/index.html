The claim

Apple is evaluating ways to mass-produce high-mobility oxide (HMO) TFT backplanes for future OLED panels, possibly as a successor to LTPO, with Samsung Display and LG Display favoring a cheaper sputtering process over atomic layer deposition.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Korean outlet The Elec, relayed by MacRumors; analyst Ross Young says HMO is already used in the new touchscreen MacBook Pro. The Elec has good supply-chain sources but its timelines often slip, and the report names no product or date: MacBooks and iMacs are only listed as possible uses. Apple has said nothing.
