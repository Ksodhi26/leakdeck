The claim

After an October beta, Apple will roll out Apple Intelligence in China with iOS 27.3 in December, without the new Siri, Image Playground or the newer Genmoji.

Why we rated it Confirmed

Reported by Bloomberg. The groundwork is confirmed: Tim Cook said on Oct 15 that a beta arrives this month after regulators approved the first features and Alibaba's Qwen models, and the SCMP reports China's MIIT granted network permits for the models. Apple has not given a date or feature list for the full launch. Update Oct 31: Apple confirmed it. Its China Newsroom says the features enter developer beta on Nov 4 and launch with iOS 27.3 in December; Siri AI and Image Playground are excluded, as Bloomberg reported, though Genmoji is in the first wave. The October beta Cook promised did not happen. Stays active until iOS 27.3 actually ships.
