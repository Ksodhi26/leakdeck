Apple launches an OLED iPad Air, a vapor-chamber iPad Pro and an A19 base iPad in the first half of 2027

The claim

Apple will release three iPads in the first half of 2027: an iPad Air with an OLED screen, an iPad Pro with a new chip and vapor-chamber cooling, and a base iPad with the A19, 8GB of RAM (enabling Apple Intelligence), the N1 wireless chip and the C1X modem.

Why we rated it Likely

From Bloomberg's Mark Gurman (Power On, Nov 1), who called this autumn's MacBook Pro, iPad mini and iMac line-up correctly. Chip choices beyond the base iPad's A19 (M5 for the Air, M6 or later for the Pro) are outlets' expectations, not part of the report. One strong source and no supply-chain confirmation yet, so it stays a Rumor. Update Nov 6: Korean industry sources (via GSMArena and SamMobile) independently put the first OLED iPad Air in the first half of 2027, with Samsung Display supplying most panels alongside BOE and 4-5 million Air units forecast for 2027. Supply-chain backing for the headline OLED Air moves this to Likely; the iPad Pro and base iPad details are still Gurman-only.
