The claim

Apple will announce OLED touchscreen 14- and 16-inch MacBook Pros, an OLED iPad mini, a 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro and an M6 iMac on or around Oct 27, with a media day in Los Angeles on Oct 28.

Why we rated it Likely

Reported by Bloomberg and matched Apple's earlier pattern of two October launch rounds (the Oct 13 event happened as reported). On Oct 15 Gurman added that media were invited to an all-day launch experience on Oct 28. Apple has not confirmed. Reports disagree on whether the OLED models use M6 or M5 Pro/Max chips, and on availability dates.
