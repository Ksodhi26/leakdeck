The claim

At its Oct 28 launch Apple will announce OLED touchscreen 14- and 16-inch MacBook Pros (M5 Pro/Max), an OLED iPad mini, a 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro and an M6 iMac, with sales from Nov 6.

Why we rated it Likely

The date is now official: on Oct 19 Apple's Greg Joswiak posted the invite for a 'Pro and tell' media experience in Los Angeles on Oct 28 at 10 a.m. PT, one day after Bloomberg's 'on or around Oct 27'. The tagline points to the MacBook Pro, but Apple has not named any product, so the line-up remains a Bloomberg report. Supporting evidence: Bloomberg called the Oct 13 launch correctly; an unreleased iPad mini Smart Folio (A3632) appeared on Apple's French regulatory site; and Gurman says Apple Stores plan an overnight reset on Nov 5-6. The chip question has largely settled: Gurman, 9to5Mac and MacRumors now all say the OLED models use the existing M5 Pro/Max, with M6 only in the entry 14-inch MacBook Pro and iMac. Update Oct 26: part of the claim is already confirmed. Apple announced the OLED iPad mini (8.5-inch, A20 Pro) by press release, with pre-orders Oct 28 and availability Nov 6, matching the reported Nov 6 sales date. Greg Joswiak then teased an 'exciting week', and Gurman's Oct 25 newsletter repeats that the touchscreen MacBook Pros come Wednesday. The MacBook Pro and iMac parts are still unannounced. We will resolve this on Oct 28.
