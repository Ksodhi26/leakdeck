The claim

Apple will release a HomePod mini 2 with red mesh and a silver AirPods Max 2 with white cushions and headband and a red Digital Crown, plus red AirPods Wrist Strap and Apple Watch Sport Band, tied to World AIDS Day on Dec 1.

Why we rated it Likely

Reported by Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, who has a strong Apple record, and relayed by MacRumors and The Verge. Apple has sold no (PRODUCT)RED devices since early 2025, so this would be a revival. One source and a date that is only reported, so it is a Rumor until Apple announces it; we will check after Dec 1. Update Nov 25: Gurman added in a follow-up post that Apple will also release (PRODUCT)RED versions of the AirPods Pro Wrist Strap and the Apple Watch Sport Band, making four products (MacRumors). Still a single-source report; stays Rumor until Apple announces. Also Nov 25: Apple itself has scheduled a (RED) 20th-anniversary session at its SoHo store in New York on Dec 1 at 12 p.m. ET with marketing chief Greg Joswiak (MacRumors), which fits the reported date, and 9to5Mac separately heard of a (PRODUCT)RED AirTag. With Apple marking the day officially, this moves from Rumor to Likely; the products themselves are still unannounced.
