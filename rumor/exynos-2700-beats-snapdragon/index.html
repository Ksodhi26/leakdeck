The claim

In Samsung's internal tests the Exynos 2700 for the Galaxy S27 beat the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max (Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6) by about 20% in Geekbench 6.5 multi-core and posted 5-20% higher GPU results.

Why we rated it Rumor

A single report from Korea's Chosun Biz, relayed by Android Authority. It covers only multi-core CPU and unnamed peak GPU tests against one Xiaomi phone, with nothing on single-core speed, sustained performance, heat or battery life, and the numbers come from Samsung's own testing. A 10-core Exynos winning multi-core against an 8-core chip is plausible, but the headline 'beats Snapdragon' needs independent benchmarks.
