The claim

The Galaxy A38's battery is rated at 5,882mAh, likely marketed as 6,000mAh, up from the 5,000mAh the A30 line has used since the Galaxy A31; charging likely stays at 45W.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Dutch site Galaxy Club, which often reports Samsung battery ratings from certification and supply data, relayed by Android Authority and SamMobile. It fits reports of bigger batteries across Samsung's 2027 lineup. The 45W figure is an inference from the A37 and A58, not part of the leak. Samsung has said nothing.
