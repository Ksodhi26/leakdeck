The claim

The global Galaxy A38 (SM-A386B) uses the MediaTek Dimensity 7500 instead of an Exynos chip, with at least a 6GB RAM version, running Android 17 with One UI 9.

Why we rated it Likely

A Geekbench listing for SM-A386B (928 single-core, 2,687 multi-core) shows the chip, reported by GSMArena. Benchmark listings are usually reliable for chipsets, but this is a prototype and Samsung sometimes uses different chips by region, so Likely rather than Confirmed.
