Samsung readies a Galaxy Fit 4 that looks just like the Fit 3

Rating

The claim

Samsung will launch a Galaxy Fit 4 (SM-R391) fitness band with a near-identical design to the Fit 3, in black, white and light blue, at a similar low price.

Why we rated it Likely

Android Authority obtained 360-degree videos of the band in three colors and earlier found Samsung documents naming SM-R391 as 'FIT4'; a Samsung app teardown also showed renders. The hardware is well evidenced, but specs beyond Bluetooth LE and a Goodix optical sensor, the price (the Fit 3 was about $60) and the launch date are unknown; reports range from late 2026 to early 2027. Update Oct 30: a SammyGuru teardown of the Galaxy Fit 4 Manager app, reported by Android Authority, lists nearly the same health features as the Fit 3 (sleep coaching, SpO2, snoring detection, auto workout tracking), the same tile-based UI with up to 12 tiles, and 258 x 428 watch-face assets that suggest a slightly taller screen.
