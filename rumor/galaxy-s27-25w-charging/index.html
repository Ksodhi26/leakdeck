The base Galaxy S27 stays at 25W wired charging

The claim

The standard Galaxy S27 will charge at 25W wired, the same as the Galaxy S26, while the S27 Pro and Ultra get 60W.

Why we rated it Likely

Hard evidence from certifications: a China 3C listing for model SM-S9520 last month showed 25W, and on Oct 29 GSMArena reported UL Solutions (Demko) certificates for several variants rated 9V/2.77A (24.93W). The 60W figure for the Pro and Ultra comes from separate 3C filings reported by smaller sites. Certifications are reliable on charging limits, but Samsung has not announced the phones.

Sources

  • GSMArena
  • SammyGuru (Pro/Ultra 60W)
