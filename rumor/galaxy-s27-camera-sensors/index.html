The claim

The Galaxy S27 and S27+ keep a 50MP main, 12MP ultrawide and 10MP 3x camera; the S27 Pro and Ultra share a 200MP ISOCELL HP6 main, a 50MP ultrawide and a 16MP selfie camera, but the Pro gets only a 10MP 3x telephoto and the Ultra a single 50MP 5x telephoto.

Why we rated it Rumor

From SammyGuru, relayed by Android Authority and GSMArena, with specific sensor part numbers. It fits Ice Universe's August claim that the Ultra has only three rear cameras, and on Nov 10 he said the fourth camera shown on dummy units shared by Sonny Dickson 'does not exist'. The Pro's 10MP 3x camera contradicts earlier leaks of 50MP 5x or 3.5x options. One sourced leak, Samsung has said nothing, so it is a Rumor.
