The claim

Samsung will offer the Galaxy S27 series in five colors matching leaked stock wallpapers: white/cream, dark blue, light blue, pink and burgundy.

Why we rated it Rumor

Based on five color-matched stock wallpapers from what SammyGuru says are leaked firmware files, relayed by SamMobile, Android Authority and Android Police. Samsung usually ships wallpapers that match its phone colors, but wallpapers are not a color list, which models get which colors is unknown, and Samsung has said nothing.
