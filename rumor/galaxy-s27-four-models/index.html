The claim

Samsung will release the Galaxy S27, S27+, S27 Pro and S27 Ultra.

Why we rated it Likely

Model numbers for all four (SM-S952U, SM-S956U, SM-S958U and a global dual-SIM SM-S957B/DS) appeared in the GSMA IMEI database, which is strong evidence a phone exists but does not confirm names, specs or launch dates. On Oct 16, two more model numbers (SM-S9570 and SM-S9580, believed to be the Pro and Ultra) appeared in China's MIIT certification database. On Oct 20 leaked third-party screen protectors showed only the S27, S27+ and S27 Ultra (6.27, 6.66 and 6.89 inches), without the Pro. Accessory leaks are less reliable than certification databases, so we keep this at Likely for now.
