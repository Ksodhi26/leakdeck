The claim

Samsung will not release new Galaxy Watch models in 2027, ending yearly launches that began in 2018; the Galaxy Watch 9 and Watch Ultra 2 stay current until 2028, existing watches get software and health updates, and a new Galaxy Fit band fills the gap.

Why we rated it Rumor

Reported by Korea's ETNews from industry sources and relayed by GSMArena, SamMobile and Android Authority. It is a cost story: Samsung's mobile division reportedly lost money for two quarters and the Galaxy Watch 9 sold below expectations after a price rise. The Galaxy Fit 4 part fits Samsung's own new mention of that band. One outlet and Samsung has not confirmed, so Rumor.
