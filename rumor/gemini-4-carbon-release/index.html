The claim

Google's next Gemini 4 checkpoint, Carbon, is in partner early access and will be released publicly soon.

Why we rated it Likely

Business Insider first reported Carbon inside Google's internal coding tool, The Information said it is coming this month, testers report it on LMArena under Google's early access program, and Carbon 'teamfood' model IDs showed up in a Google server archive. That is strong evidence it exists and is close, but Google has not announced it and the date could slip into November. Early testers say it is better than Argon at 3D work but much slower.
