The claim

Apple's HomeView hub will allow third-party conversational AI apps such as ChatGPT and Claude.

Why we rated it Likely

Leaker pdfu found an unused ConversationalApps flag earlier this month and says iOS 27.3 beta 1 adds a Conversational Apps category to PebblePlay, HomeView's CarPlay-style app bridge, along with two-way audio routing and session handling (reported by 9to5Mac). Apple uses these category flags to decide which third-party apps can run, so this is hard evidence of intent. It is still 'not fully wired up', and Apple has not named any apps or a date.
