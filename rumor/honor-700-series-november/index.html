The claim

Honor will launch three Honor 700 models in China next month; the 700 Pro has a 6.57-inch 1.5K screen, a 200MP main camera and a 1.72-inch rear display.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Weibo tipsters relayed by GSMArena: one says the event is next month and the series has three models; Digital Chat Station has repeated the 200MP camera and rear screen since August. The timing fits Honor's six-month number-series cadence (the 600 launched in May), but Honor has announced nothing.
