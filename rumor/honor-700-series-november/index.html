Honor 700 series launches in November with three models and a rear screen on the Pro

Rating
Likely
Status
Active
Expected
Early November 2026 (China, leak; no date)
First reported
Oct 21, 2026
Last updated
Nov 4, 2026
Categories
Google / Android

The claim

Honor will launch three Honor 700 models in China in November; the 700 Pro has a 6.57-inch 1.5K screen, a 200MP main camera, a 50MP periscope telephoto, a 1.72-inch built-in rear display and a battery of about 9,000mAh.

Why we rated it Likely

From Weibo tipsters relayed by GSMArena: one says the event is next month and the series has three models; Digital Chat Station has repeated the 200MP camera and rear screen since August. The timing fits Honor's six-month number-series cadence (the 600 launched in May), but Honor has announced nothing. On Oct 24 Digital Chat Station added details he says come from an engineering prototype (codename 'Flora'): a roughly 9,000mAh battery, a 50MP periscope on the Pro and four test colors, while calling the November timing tentative. More detail from the same tipster, so it stays a Rumor. Update Oct 28-Nov 1: two Honor 700 models (FAS-AN00 and FAA-AN00, thought to be the 700 and 700 Pro) passed China's 3C certification, with a tipster saying a third is pending, which usually means a launch within weeks. Tipster Dingjiao Shuma (via Kuai Keji, Oct 31) says the series launches in early November with actress Ju Jingyi as spokesperson, a Snapdragon chip, a periscope and support for a 200mm teleconverter. Certification plus several tipsters agreeing lifts the November timing to Likely; the 9,000mAh battery and exact specs still rest mainly on Digital Chat Station, and Honor has not announced a date. Update Nov 3: Honor CMO Guan Haitao officially announced the Honor 700 series on Weibo, confirmed the 'Flora' codename and said it debuts 'in winter', without a date. Tipster Wangzai Baishitong says the three models (700, 700 Pro and a Vitality Edition) launch this month. The series is now official; the November date and specs are still unconfirmed, so it stays Likely. Update Nov 3: Honor's global CMO Guan Haitao officially announced the Honor 700 series (codename Flora) for a 'winter' debut, and Honor named actress Ju Jingyi as ambassador. A tipster says three models (700, 700 Pro, and a Vitality Edition) are 3C certified and launch this month. Honor still has not given a date, so the November timing stays Likely.
