The claim

Honor's next book-style foldable, the Magic V7, uses a wide form factor, and Honor won't release a conventional tall foldable alongside it.

Why we rated it Rumor

From a tipster on X, reported by GSMArena. An earlier leak described an Honor wide foldable with a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6, a 7.6-inch inner screen, a 5.5-inch cover screen and over 7,000mAh from CNY 9,999, but it did not name the model. Honor has not announced anything.
