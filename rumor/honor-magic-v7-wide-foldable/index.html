Honor Magic V7 is a wide foldable, with no regular-shaped Magic V model next year

Rating

Last updated

The claim

Honor's next book-style foldable, the Magic V7, uses a wide form factor, and Honor won't release a conventional tall foldable alongside it.

Why we rated it Likely

From a tipster on X, reported by GSMArena. An earlier leak described an Honor wide foldable with a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6, a 7.6-inch inner screen, a 5.5-inch cover screen and over 7,000mAh from CNY 9,999, but it did not name the model. Honor has not announced anything. Update Nov 21: Digital Chat Station says Honor's wide foldable, which he links to the Magic V7 name only via earlier rumors, will most likely launch after Chinese New Year in early February, and that a non-folding wide phone is tentatively planned for the first half of 2027 (GSMArena). A second leaker supports a wide foldable existing, but the name is still unconfirmed. Stays Rumor. Update Nov 24 (re-rated Rumor -> Likely): at the Honor 700 launch, CEO Li Jian reportedly said Honor's first wide foldable will debut in March 2027 with a crease-free display, and urged buyers considering the iPhone Duo to wait (GSMArena). A company statement that a wide foldable is coming makes the core claim Likely. The Magic V7 name and the claim that no conventional foldable comes alongside are still unconfirmed, and March is later than the 'early February' leak.
