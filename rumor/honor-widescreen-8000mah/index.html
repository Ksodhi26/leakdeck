The claim

A wide-screen phone with a 6.39-inch display, a Snapdragon chip, a 200MP main camera, a periscope telephoto and at least 8,000mAh of battery is in development, apparently from Honor.

Why we rated it Shaky

Digital Chat Station posted the specs on Weibo but did not name the brand; GSMArena reads Honor from the emoji he used. The specs may be solid, but the brand is a guess and the device is an early engineering sample, so this is Shaky. It joins similar wide-screen reports about Oppo, vivo, Xiaomi, OnePlus and Huawei's OSTA.
