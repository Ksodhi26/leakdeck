The claim

Honor's Win 2 and Win 2 RT phones launch this month with 100W/90W charging and 10,000mAh+ batteries.

Why we rated it Confirmed

Honor announced on Oct 12 that the Win 2 series would launch Oct 21, and it did: the Win 2 arrived in China with a 10,100mAh battery and 100W wired charging, so the battery and 100W parts of the claim held up. Honor launched the Win RT 2 and Win Turbo 2 alongside it, but we could not verify the 90W charging figure the original leak gave for the other models.

Outcome

Confirmed: the Win 2 launched in China on Oct 21 with a 10,100mAh battery and 100W charging. The 90W claim for other models is unverified.
