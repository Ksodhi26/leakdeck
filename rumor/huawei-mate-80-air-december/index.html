Huawei Mate 80 Air launches in December

Rating

The claim

Huawei will launch the Mate 80 Air thin phone next month, with the MatePad Mini 2 arriving separately, possibly also in December.

Why we rated it Confirmed

A single Weibo tipster via GSMArena. Another tipster said a few days ago that a Mate-series Air arrives within two months with 12GB of RAM and a circular camera island; the Kirin 9030 and curved screen are expected but unconfirmed. The Mate 70 Air launched about a year ago, so the timing is plausible, but Huawei has said nothing. Update Nov 19: Huawei officially announced a thin Mate phone for its Nov 24 event, but it is called the Mate 90 Air, and Vmall reservations are open with 12GB of RAM and 256GB/512GB storage.

Outcome

Partly correct. Huawei's thin Mate phone is real and launches before the end of the year, but it is called the Mate 90 Air (Huawei skipped the 'Mate 80 Air' name) and it is unveiled on Nov 24, about a week before the December window. The 12GB of RAM and round camera island from the second tipster were right.
