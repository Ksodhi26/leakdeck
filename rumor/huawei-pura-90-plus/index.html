The claim

Huawei is preparing a Pura 90 Plus with a 50MP 1-inch-type RYYB variable-aperture main camera in a triple setup, a 6.8-inch 1.5K screen and four colors.

Why we rated it Likely

The phone itself is now official: on Oct 23 Huawei opened Pura 90 Plus pre-orders in China for an Oct 28 sale and showed four colors (matching the tipster's 'four colors') and up to 12GB/1TB. The camera and screen specs still come only from Weibo tipster Digital Chat Station, who has a decent record on Chinese phones, and Huawei has not published them. We will resolve this on Oct 28.
