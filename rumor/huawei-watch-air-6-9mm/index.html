The claim

Huawei's upcoming Watch Air has a 6.9mm titanium unibody, an irregular high-silicon stacked battery (over 10% silicon) for up to five days of use, Watch GT 7 Pro-level activity tracking and Watch 6 sensors including ECG.

Why we rated it Likely

From Weibo tipster Fixed Focus Digital, relayed by GSMArena with leaked images. The name and thin design have been rumored before, but the specs come from one leaker and Huawei has not announced the watch or a date. Update Nov 19: Huawei's official teaser confirms the Watch Air name, the 6.9mm thickness and a titanium unibody, with launch on Nov 24. The battery is still unconfirmed, and Digital Chat Station now says about a week rather than five days, so the claim moves to Likely until launch.
