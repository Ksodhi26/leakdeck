The claim

A 16-core Nova Lake-H chip (8 P-cores, 4 E-cores, 4 low-power E-cores) runs up to 4.8GHz turbo and 2.0GHz base with 18MB of last-level cache, a 2.3GHz iGPU, DDR5-8000 support up to 128GB and a 25W TDP.

Why we rated it Likely

Leaker @momomo_us found the figures on a spec sheet for an Advantech industrial board that names 'Core Ultra Series 4' and 'Nova Lake-H' (via Wccftech). A board maker's spec sheet is solid evidence, but it doesn't name the SKU and consumer laptop parts may be clocked or configured differently. Likely.
