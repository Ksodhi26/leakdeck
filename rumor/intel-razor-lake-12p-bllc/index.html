The claim

Intel's 2028 Razor Lake-S lineup includes an unlocked '12+0+4' chip with 12 P-cores, four low-power E-cores and Intel's big Last Level Cache (bLLC), built on Intel 18A-P, replacing a planned 8+0+4 part; a 6+12+4 bLLC chip moves from Nova Lake to Razor Lake.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Intel leaker Jaykihn on X, reported by TechPowerUp. He has a decent record on Intel roadmaps, and Intel has listed Razor Lake model IDs in its ISA reference, but none of these configurations are official and plans two years out often change. One source, so it is a Rumor.
