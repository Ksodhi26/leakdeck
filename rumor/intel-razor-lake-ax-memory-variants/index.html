The claim

Intel plans two Razor Lake-AX lines: memory-on-package up to 96GB and memory-on-motherboard up to 192GB, with up to 8P+16E+4LPE cores, 32 Xe3 GPU cores and 16 PCIe 5.0 lanes.

Why we rated it Rumor

From leaker Jaykihn, relayed by VideoCardz, which separately saw a four-memory-chip prototype and says it has heard of a 128GB option. Jaykihn is usually well informed on Intel roadmaps, but the product line is said to be still being defined and Intel has said nothing. Rumor.
