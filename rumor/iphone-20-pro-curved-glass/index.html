The 'iPhone 20 Pro' uses iPhone Air-style glass that curves on all four sides

The claim

Apple's 2027 anniversary Pro iPhones use the same glass material as the iPhone Air, shaped with more curvature so the front and back curve on all four edges around a thin polished metal frame.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Weibo leaker Fixed Focus Digital, relayed by MacRumors, who earlier said Apple modified iPhone Air production equipment to make more rounded glass. The broad direction matches Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, who has reported glass curving into the sides on the anniversary Pro models. The material detail and the 'iPhone 20 Pro' name rest on one leaker, and Apple's naming is unknown, so it is a Rumor. Update Nov 17: Ice Universe posted videos of an 'approved' quad-curved cover glass sample and screen protectors with very thin borders and a Dynamic Island cutout (MacRumors, GSMArena). MacRumors notes such parts are often early manufacturing tests built from rumors rather than Apple's final specs, and they say nothing about the glass material, so this stays a Rumor.
