The claim

Apple lowered quality inspection standards for the iPhone Duo to get more units to stores before the holidays, as it targets 7.5 million shipments by year end.

Why we rated it Shaky

The claim comes from one unnamed Foxconn employee quoted by China's Jiemian News and relayed by GSMArena. Sina reports another supply-chain source who denies Apple lowered its yield standards, and the reports themselves say a batch of components was replaced. Apple has acknowledged inner-display damage on 'a small number' of units and offers replacements, but has said nothing about inspection standards. A single anonymous source with a direct denial makes this Shaky.
