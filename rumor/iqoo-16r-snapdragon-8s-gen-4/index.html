The claim

The vivo I2606, believed to be the iQOO 16R or iQOO 16s, runs a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 with 12GB of RAM on Android 17, a step down from the iQOO 15R's Snapdragon 8 Gen 5.

Why we rated it Likely

A Geekbench listing for the I2606 shows the SM8735 (Snapdragon 8s Gen 4), 12GB of RAM and Android 17 (GSMArena, Gizmochina). The model has passed India's BIS, Malaysia's SIRIM and Indonesia's SDPPI, with the IMEI database calling it the 16R and SIRIM/SDPPI the 16s. The chip is hard evidence; the final name and other specs are not. Likely.
