The claim

Lenovo's first detachable Googlebook, the Googlebook Duet 13, with a 13-inch screen, a detachable keyboard, a stylus and a kickstand cover, launches in January 2027.

Why we rated it Likely

From Evan Blass, who shared new images and said it is 'launching in January' (via 9to5Google and Android Authority). Google has already teased detachable Googlebooks as coming soon, and Blass's leaked images and track record are strong, so the product looks real and the timing is Likely. Lenovo and Google have not announced a date, name or price.
