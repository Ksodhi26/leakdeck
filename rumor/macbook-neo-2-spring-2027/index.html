The claim

Apple's second MacBook Neo moves from the A18 Pro with 8GB of RAM to the A19 Pro with 12GB and launches in the iOS/macOS 27.4 window (February-March 2027), possibly in new colors; Apple plans to refresh the Neo's colors often.

Why we rated it Likely

From Bloomberg's Mark Gurman in his Nov 22 Power On newsletter (via 9to5Mac and GSMArena), matching his September report of an A19 Pro, 12GB Neo in 2027. Gurman has a strong record on Apple timing, but Apple has said nothing and the date could fall at either end of the window. Likely.
