Motorola readies a 'Moto Tag 3' that is a Moto Tag 2 in new colors

The claim

Motorola will sell a Moto Tag 3 tracker in Lucent White and Indigo Blue with the same UWB, Find Hub support, IP rating, CR2032 battery (500+ days) and 8.5g weight as the Moto Tag 2.

Why we rated it Likely

Retail listings appeared on Amazon UK, Amazon Italy and Italian store InItPC (spotted by @matteventu), and Evan Blass shared official-looking images, per Android Authority and 9to5Google. Multiple retailer listings make a launch Likely. Motorola has not commented, and it's unclear whether 'Moto Tag 3' is the final name or just a color refresh of the Tag 2, which launched only this year.
