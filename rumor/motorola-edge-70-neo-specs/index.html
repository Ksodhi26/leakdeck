Motorola Edge 70 Neo has a Snapdragon 7s Gen 3, 7,000mAh battery and 144Hz screen

The claim

The Edge 70 Neo will have a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED, Snapdragon 7s Gen 3, 50MP Sony LYTIA 710 main camera, 32MP selfie camera, 7,000mAh battery with 45W charging and IP64.

Why we rated it Confirmed

The phone itself is real: Motorola accidentally teased it in August and renders followed. The spec list comes from a single leak reported by GSMArena with no certification or official backing yet. Motorola has not announced a launch date. Update Nov 18: Motorola's Flipkart teaser sets an India launch for Nov 24 and shows '50MP', 'OIS' and 'Lytia' on the camera ring, matching the leaked 50MP Lytia main camera (earlier renders showed 200MP). The chip, battery and screen are still unconfirmed, so it stays a Rumor until the launch. Update Nov 19: Motorola has now confirmed the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3, a 6.8-inch 144Hz AMOLED, the 50MP Lytia 710 main camera, a 7,000mAh battery with 45W charging and IP64, matching the leak. Only the 32MP selfie camera is unconfirmed; we will resolve this after the Nov 24 launch.
