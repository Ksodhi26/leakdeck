The claim

The base Razr 2027 (codename Raptor; likely Razr 80 outside North America) gets a 4-inch 120Hz cover screen, a 6.9-inch 144Hz main screen, a Dimensity 7550X, 8GB/256GB, a 5,000mAh battery with 45W wired and 15W wireless charging, IPX8/IPX9, Android 17 with three OS updates, 14.6mm folded and 195g, and launches in January, months earlier than usual.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Android Headlines' images and spec sheet, relayed by 9to5Google and GSMArena. An earlier Geekbench run of a Razr with a MediaTek chip fits, but Motorola has not commented and a January launch would break its spring pattern. One leak with images: Rumor.
