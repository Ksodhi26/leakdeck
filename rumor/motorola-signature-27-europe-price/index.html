The claim

The Motorola Signature 27 (12GB/512GB) will cost €1,594.90 in Europe, up nearly 60% from the first Signature's €999, and the unannounced Razr Flex 27 (12GB/256GB) will cost €1,510.90.

Why we rated it Rumor

The prices come from listings on Portuguese retailer MegaPC. Retailer listings are real evidence but often use placeholder prices: the Razr Flex listings have no photos, one wrongly describes a clamshell, and all are out of stock with no ship date. Motorola has not confirmed European or US pricing.
