Motorola Signature 27 costs about €1,595 in Europe, and a Razr Flex 27 about €1,511

The claim

The Motorola Signature 27 (12GB/512GB) will cost €1,594.90 in Europe, up nearly 60% from the first Signature's €999, and the unannounced Razr Flex 27 (12GB/256GB) will cost €1,510.90.

Why we rated it Debunked

The prices come from listings on Portuguese retailer MegaPC. Retailer listings are real evidence but often use placeholder prices: the Razr Flex listings have no photos, one wrongly describes a clamshell, and all are out of stock with no ship date. Motorola has not confirmed European or US pricing. Update Oct 29: Motorola's US and India pages say the Signature 27 arrives in November, which fits the retailer timing, but it still has not confirmed any price. Update Nov 5: Motorola announced the Signature 27 with a recommended European price of EUR 1,899, not EUR 1,595, going on sale Nov 16; GSMArena says some retailers and partners will sell it at EUR 1,399. The US price is $1,599. The Razr Flex 27 is still unannounced.

Outcome

Wrong. The leaked EUR 1,594.90 retailer price matched neither of Motorola's figures: the official recommended price is EUR 1,899, with some partners at EUR 1,399. The 'nearly 60% more than the first Signature' framing understated the official jump. The Razr Flex 27 price remains unverifiable because the phone has not been announced.
