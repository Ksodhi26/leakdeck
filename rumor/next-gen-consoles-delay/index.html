The claim

Sony's PS6 and Microsoft's next Xbox (Project Helix) will launch later than planned because of high memory prices.

Why we rated it Likely

Sony's CEO said the PS6 launch date is still undecided because of the component shortage, and Microsoft's new CEO admitted the next Xbox is affected. The 2028-2029 timeframe for PS6 is a report, not a statement. On Oct 20 Fortune reported, citing people familiar with Microsoft's plans, that the Project Helix console is planned for 2028, with a next-gen Xbox handheld possibly by the end of 2027; see our separate price rumor.
