The claim

Graphics card makers in China will get 20-30% fewer Nvidia gaming GPUs from November to January, after an already tight Q3, making consumer GPU prices volatile.

Why we rated it Rumor

From a Board Channels supply-chain post reported by VideoCardz and relayed by TechRadar. Board Channels is an anonymous forum with a mixed record, the claim covers only China, and Nvidia has not commented. It fits the memory squeeze and Nvidia's AI focus, but it is unverified. Rumor.
