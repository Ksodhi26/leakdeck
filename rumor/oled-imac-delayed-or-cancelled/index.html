Apple may delay or cancel its OLED iMac

The claim

Apple is talking to Samsung Display about delaying, or possibly shelving, the OLED iMac because of high panel costs and weak OLED demand, and has asked LCD makers including BOE for panels for an iMac around 2029.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Korea's The Elec citing industry sources, relayed by MacRumors and 9to5Mac. One source said talks focus on a delay rather than cancellation. It fits weak OLED iPad Pro sales and rising memory costs, but in October Mark Gurman said Apple was still testing OLED for the iMac and MacBook Air. Single-outlet supply-chain report; Apple never announced an OLED iMac.
