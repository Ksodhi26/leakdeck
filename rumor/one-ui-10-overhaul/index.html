The claim

Samsung's One UI 10 will be its biggest software overhaul yet, built around cross-app 'contextual flow', fewer menus and tighter device handoffs, possibly with paid tiers for heavy AI features.

Why we rated it Rumor

Comes from an unnamed insider via Nokia Power User, relayed by GSMArena; tipster Ice Universe, who has a solid Samsung track record, says his own source confirms 'significant changes, unprecedented in nature'. Timing is disputed: SamMobile expects mid-2027 with the Galaxy Z Fold 9 and Flip 9, while GSMArena's reading of the insider points to the Galaxy S29. Samsung has said nothing, and the details are vague.
