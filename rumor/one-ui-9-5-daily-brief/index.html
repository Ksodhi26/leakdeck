One UI 9.5 adds a 'Daily Brief' that summarizes your emails and app notifications

Rating
Likely

Last updated

Categories

The claim

Samsung's Now Brief gains a Daily Brief that pulls notifications from apps such as WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, KakaoTalk and SMS/RCS plus your email, and sorts them into cards for finance and payments, bookings and appointments, and work email at a set time of day.

Why we rated it Likely

From an Android Authority APK teardown of Samsung's Personal Agent Core package, with developer strings naming the feature and its card categories. Code evidence shows Samsung is building it, but Android Authority stresses the code is work in progress and may not ship, and it suggests these agent features may sit in a paid Galaxy AI tier. Samsung has said nothing, so it is a Rumor. Update Nov 18: SamMobile published screenshots of the feature working (as 'Daily Briefing'), with reminder cards built from payment notices, maintenance fees, appointments and email deadlines, grouped into categories such as Finance, Work and Productivity, and Mobility and Traffic (relayed by Android Authority). SamMobile also showed a related Recall card that pulls reservations and tickets from Gmail. Screenshots of a working build from a second outlet move this to Likely. It is not in the first One UI 9.5 beta changelog, it may launch with the Galaxy S27 instead, and it appears on a leaked list of paid Galaxy AI Premium features.
