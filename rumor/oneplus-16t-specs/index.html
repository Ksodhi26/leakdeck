The claim

A compact Oppo-made phone will launch outside China as the OnePlus 16T with a 6.35-inch 1.5K 120Hz LTPO screen, MediaTek's 3nm Dimensity 9600M, a 200MP main camera and a 200MP periscope, a metal frame under 9mm and a battery of about 7,000mAh with 80W charging.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Digital Chat Station on Weibo, relayed by tipster Debayan Roy and Gadgets360. The tipster has a good record on Chinese phones, but the OnePlus naming is his interpretation and neither OnePlus nor MediaTek (which has not announced a Dimensity 9600M) has said anything. The OnePlus 15T launched in China in March with Snapdragon and dual 50MP cameras, so the chip switch would be a notable change.
