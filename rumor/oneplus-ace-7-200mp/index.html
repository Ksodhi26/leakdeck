The claim

Both OnePlus Ace 7 models will use a 200MP main camera, and the Pro adds a 50MP 3.5x periscope.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Weibo tipster Digital Chat Station, relayed by GSMArena. OnePlus has now set the launch for Oct 29 and confirmed the chips (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 'V series' for the Ace 7, standard 8 Elite Gen 5 for the Pro) and the cooling fan, but it still has not confirmed camera specs. We will resolve this at launch.
