The claim

Both OnePlus Ace 7 models will use a 200MP main camera, and the Pro adds a 50MP 3.5x periscope.

Why we rated it Confirmed

From Weibo tipster Digital Chat Station, relayed by GSMArena. OnePlus has now set the launch for Oct 29 and confirmed the chips (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 'V series' for the Ace 7, standard 8 Elite Gen 5 for the Pro) and the cooling fan, and on Oct 26 it confirmed the Pro half of the claim: a 200MP main camera plus a 3.5x periscope telephoto on the Ace 7 Pro (it did not state the periscope's resolution). The standard Ace 7's camera is still unconfirmed. We will resolve this at launch. Update Oct 29: OnePlus launched both phones with a 200MP main camera; the Pro adds a 50MP 3.5x periscope.

Outcome

True. Both the Ace 7 and Ace 7 Pro launched on Oct 29 with 200MP main cameras, and the Pro has a 50MP 3.5x periscope telephoto, exactly as Digital Chat Station said.
