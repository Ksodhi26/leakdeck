The claim

OnePlus is developing a Turbo Pro (possibly Turbo 7 Pro) with a new 3nm Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro (SM8845 Pro), a 6.78-inch 1.5K 185Hz screen, a 200MP main camera and a 9,000mAh battery, launching around the end of the year.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Digital Chat Station, a prolific Weibo tipster with a decent record, in a post he later edited; Android Authority reported it. Qualcomm has not announced a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro, and OnePlus has not mentioned the phone. The timing is vague.
