OnePlus readies a Turbo 7 Pro with an unreleased Snapdragon 'Pro' chip and 100W charging

Categories

The claim

OnePlus is developing a Turbo Pro (possibly Turbo 7 Pro) with a new 3nm Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro (SM8845 Pro), a 6.78-inch 1.5K 185Hz screen, a 200MP main camera and a 9,000mAh battery, launching around the end of the year.

Why we rated it Likely

From Digital Chat Station, a prolific Weibo tipster with a decent record, in a post he later edited; Android Authority reported it. Qualcomm has not announced a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro, and OnePlus has not mentioned the phone. The timing is vague. GSMArena adds that the tipster also mentioned a clip-on external fan accessory, and that the screen, camera and battery match the OnePlus 16; the same SM8845 Pro chip is rumored for the iQOO Z12 Turbo/iQOO 16R. Update Nov 2: another Weibo tipster says a OnePlus Turbo 7 will use the unannounced 'Snapdragon 8 Gen 6' with a 200MP main camera and a 185Hz screen, and launch by the end of the year; GSMArena notes it may be the same phone. Similar details from a second tipster, but Qualcomm and OnePlus have announced nothing, so it stays a Rumor. Update Nov 18: a OnePlus phone with model number PYP110, which Digital Chat Station says is the Turbo 7 Pro, passed China's 3C certification with 100W chargers. DCS adds a 185Hz screen (165Hz system-wide), a 200MP main camera, a battery of at least 9,000mAh, no built-in fan and a December launch; a tipster on X lists the SM8845 Pro chip and about 10,000mAh. The certification is hard evidence that the phone exists and charges at 100W, so this moves to Likely; the chip name, battery size and date are still unconfirmed.
