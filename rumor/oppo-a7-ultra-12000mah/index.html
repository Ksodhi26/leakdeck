The claim

Oppo will launch an A7 Ultra in December with a 12,000mAh battery and a large horizontal rear camera module.

Why we rated it Rumor

The battery has hard evidence behind it: an Oppo phone numbered PNC110 appeared in China's MIIT database with a 12,000mAh cell, and updated MIIT images show a purple phone with a horizontal camera bar. The A7 Ultra name and December timing come only from tipster Digital Chat Station, who is usually reliable on Chinese phones, so linking PNC110 to the A7 Ultra is still an inference. Oppo has said nothing.
