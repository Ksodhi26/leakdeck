The claim

OPPO's Find X10 goes on sale on Oct 21.

Why we rated it Confirmed

We first rated this Shaky because the only clue was a UK promotion ending Oct 21. OPPO then confirmed an Oct 21 global launch, and on the day it published prices and opened orders in Europe (Finland: EUR 1,499 and EUR 1,799). But 'on sale' only partly held: European deliveries start Nov 2, Singapore pre-orders open Oct 22 with deliveries from Oct 26, and India launches Oct 22.

Outcome

Mostly confirmed: launched and orderable on Oct 21, but phones ship from Nov 2 in Europe, so it was not in buyers' hands that day.
