The claim

The Find X10 Ultra (model PYU110, codename 'Xinyun') uses MediaTek's Dimensity 9600 Pro instead of a Snapdragon chip.

Why we rated it Shaky

Tipster Abhishek Yadav on X and Weibo's Smart Pikachu both pointed to the Dimensity 9600 Pro, which Oppo already uses in the Find X10 Pro Max. But Smart Pikachu has since said a Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 version is now more likely, per Android Central's summary via Gadget Hacks. Conflicting signals from the same small pool of leakers; Oppo has not confirmed the phone exists.
