The claim

Oppo will launch a pocket gimbal camera, codenamed Fuyao, before the end of 2026; its wide-screen foldable (likely Find N7) with a 2nm Snapdragon chip and a 16:9 'Reno X' bar phone are also near launch.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Weibo tipster Digital Chat Station, relayed by GSMArena and Android Headlines. Earlier rumors gave the camera a 200MP 1/1.12-inch sensor and Hasselblad branding. Digital Chat Station has a decent record on Oppo, but this is one tipster with no official teaser, and the year-end timing leaves little room.
