The claim

Because of memory costs, vivo plans 60 million phones in 2027 (from 85 million), Xiaomi 90 million (from 120 million), OPPO 90 million (down 14%), Motorola 40 million (from 50 million), Samsung 210 million (from 232 million) and Apple 220 million (from 233 million); Honor keeps 50 million.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Jiemian News citing supply chain sources, relayed by Android Authority and SamMobile. It fits the documented memory squeeze: Xiaomi's Q3 results cite memory prices up two to three times this year and an industry shipment drop of about 20%, and Honor's CEO said at the 700 launch that Honor will push while others pull back. The exact unit targets come from one report and no company has confirmed them. Rumor.
