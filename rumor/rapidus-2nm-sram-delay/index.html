The claim

Japan's Rapidus failed to show working SRAM cells on its 2nm GAA process by its summer 2026 target and is fighting cleanroom contamination, which threatens its plan to start mass production in 2027.

Why we rated it Rumor

From a Bloomberg report citing people familiar, analyzed by Tom's Hardware. Rapidus has not announced any SRAM results, but it has not confirmed a delay either; it says it is on track for mass production in the second half of fiscal 2027 (ending March 2028), a later framing than its earlier '2027'. One strong outlet, company pushback. Rumor.
