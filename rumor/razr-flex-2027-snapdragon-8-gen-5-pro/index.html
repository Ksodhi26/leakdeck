Motorola Razr Flex 2027 uses an unreleased Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro

The claim

The Razr Flex 2027 (XT2761, codename Parker), a compact wide foldable, runs Android 17 on Qualcomm's unannounced SM8845 Pro chip, with an under-display fingerprint reader and Bluetooth 6.0.

Why we rated it Likely

Android Authority cites a security certification document for the XT2761, a model number already tied to the Razr Flex 27 in UAE filings, and the phone appeared on Motorola's own website earlier this month. A certification listing is good evidence for the chip; the 'Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro' name is the expected branding, not confirmed. A rear photo from Reddit is unverified, and the in-display fingerprint contradicts earlier leaks. Update Nov 20: GSMArena also reports the TrustCB listing (SM8845 Pro, Bluetooth 6.0, face unlock, under-display fingerprint) and notes a tipster says the same chip may be branded Snapdragon 8 Gen 6 in the OnePlus Turbo 7 Pro, so the final name is still open. It expects a mid-December launch. Stays Likely. Update Nov 21: New renders show the Razr Flex in two Pantone finishes (green fabric-like and beige leather-like backs), and their source claims a US price around $1,700. Earlier leaks pointed to mid-December, but a test version of Motorola's Australian support site hinted at a 2027 debut (GSMArena). The price and timing are unverified; the chip claim stays Likely.
