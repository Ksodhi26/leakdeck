The claim

An upcoming Realme 17 Pro or 17 Pro+ has a flat 1.5K 120Hz OLED (165Hz in some games), the Dimensity 7500, a 200MP main camera, a 50MP 3.5x telephoto, a 10,000mAh battery and a plastic frame.

Why we rated it Rumor

From a single tipster on X, relayed by GSMArena, which notes it is unclear whether this is the Pro or the Pro+. Realme has announced nothing; the leak is plausible given rivals' move to 10,000mAh batteries, but unverified.
