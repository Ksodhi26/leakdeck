The claim

Model M077CE in China's 3C database is the vanilla Redmi Note 18 5G and supports 45W wired charging; earlier leaks say it is essentially a Note 17 5G with a larger battery (8,888mAh in China, 8,100mAh globally).

Why we rated it Rumor

The 3C listing itself is hard evidence that M077CE exists with 45W charging, but tying it to the Note 18 5G comes from a tipster on X, and the battery figures come from an earlier leak (GSMArena). Xiaomi has said nothing. Rumor until the name is confirmed.
