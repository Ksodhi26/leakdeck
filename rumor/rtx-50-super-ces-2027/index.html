NVIDIA RTX 50 Super cards debut at CES 2027

Rating

The claim

NVIDIA will launch the RTX 5060 Super (12GB), 5070 Super (18GB) and 5070 Ti/5080 Super (24GB) around CES 2027.

Why we rated it Shaky

Earlier reports named CES 2027, but a later September 2026 report says NVIDIA has no RTX 50 Super plans for 2027 and the refresh may not ship. The dates have moved repeatedly and the sources conflict. On Oct 12 Taiwan's BenchLife reported a 24GB RTX 5080 for Q1 2027 that would replace the 16GB card; it is unclear whether that is the rumored 5080 Super or a different plan (tracked separately below).
