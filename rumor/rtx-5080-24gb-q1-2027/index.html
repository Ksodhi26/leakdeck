NVIDIA swaps the RTX 5080 to 24GB in Q1 2027

Rating
Shaky
Status
Active
Expected
Q1 2027 (reported), now reportedly canceled
First reported
Oct 12, 2026
Last updated
Nov 11, 2026
Categories
Chips & PCsGaming

The claim

NVIDIA will release an RTX 5080 with 24GB of GDDR7 in early 2027, gradually replacing the 16GB model.

Why we rated it Shaky

Single-source report from Taiwan's BenchLife, relayed by VideoCardz and TechPowerUp. Board partners reportedly have only verbal guidance and no design documents yet, so timing is soft. Technically easy: 3GB memory chips on the same 256-bit bus. NVIDIA has not commented. Comes amid tight RTX 5090 supply, which NVIDIA says is constrained but not discontinued. Update Oct 30: Tom's Hardware found the RTX 5080 removed from Nvidia's US store after the RTX 5090, with street prices about 2.2x the $999 MSRP. That fits a product transition but is not proof of a 24GB model; Nvidia has not commented. Update Nov 3: BenchLife, the original source, now says the 'probability is extremely high' the 24GB card misses January, because board partners expected design details in October and still have none (relayed by VideoCardz and TechRadar). Q1 is still possible but the timing is softer, so it stays Rumor. Update Nov 11: Board Channels sources (via VideoCardz, Gazlog and leaker Harukaze) say NVIDIA told board partners the planned January 2027 China launch of the 24GB card is canceled because 3GB GDDR7 chips cost about three times as much as 2GB ones, and that the 16GB RTX 5080 stays in production. NVIDIA has neither confirmed nor denied either plan. With leaks now contradicting each other, this drops to Shaky; we will mark it Debunked if NVIDIA or partners confirm the cancellation.
