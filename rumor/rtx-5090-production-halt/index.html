NVIDIA has stopped making the RTX 5090

Rating
Debunked
Status
Resolved
First reported
Oct 10, 2026
Last updated
Oct 17, 2026
Categories
Chips & PCsGaming

The claim

NVIDIA has halted production of the GeForce RTX 5090 and is redirecting the GB202 chip to its professional RTX Pro cards.

Why we rated it Debunked

Real signs pointed that way: Micro Center reclassified the card 'Not Carried', many stores sold out, and NVIDIA pulled it from its own marketplace. But on Oct 16 NVIDIA's head of personal computing, Jeff Fisher, told reporters in Taipei the RTX 5090 is not end-of-life and NVIDIA will keep shipping it, while admitting supply is tight. By Oct 17 the card was listed again on NVIDIA's US store, though not in stock.

Outcome

Debunked by NVIDIA: the RTX 5090 has not been discontinued, though it remains scarce and expensive.
