The claim

The next GeForce generation will miss 2027 and reach retail in 2028; AMD will have only one RDNA 5 GPU in 2027.

Why we rated it Rumor

Based on a leaker's claims reported by Club386 and a Verge report that the lineup could miss 2027. Neither company has confirmed. On Oct 12 the same leaker, Kepler_L2, said AMD is unlikely to even announce RDNA 5 before Computex (June) 2027 and that a later 2027 reveal is more likely. AMD has published no schedule.
