The claim

NVIDIA's budget RTX 6060 will launch around 2029, well after the higher-end RTX 60 cards, and may not match the PS6's gaming performance.

Why we rated it Shaky

Leaker Kepler posted this in a NeoGAF thread, and TechRadar relayed it via Wccftech. He framed it as a guess based on NVIDIA's past habit of delaying low-end cards during shortages, not as inside information. It assumes the RTX 60 series itself lands in 2028, which is also only a rumor (see our RTX 60 entry).
