The claim

Samsung will launch ear-hook fitness earbuds, model SM-R545 and codenamed Galaxy T1, in several markets including the US.

Why we rated it Likely

The earbuds passed the FCC on Nov 4 (IDs ZCASMR545L and ZCASMR545R, case EP-QR545) after certifications in China, South Korea and Indonesia, and the filings mention a 'T1 Project'. That is strong evidence a product is coming, as Android Authority reports. The retail name, price and early-2027 timing are not confirmed; earlier leaks of a 595mAh case and a fit test come from software code.
