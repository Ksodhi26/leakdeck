The claim

Samsung's display-free Intelligent Eyewear (Android XR, Gemini, Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1) goes on sale in mid-December in South Korea and the US first, with Europe, China and Japan later; pricing is not set.

Why we rated it Rumor

From a Korean media report relayed by GSMArena. It fits an earlier Korean report quoting a Samsung official on a November launch, and Samsung's playbook for the Galaxy XR headset (Korea and US first). Samsung has shown the glasses and their Gentle Monster and Warby Parker frames but has not given a date or price.
