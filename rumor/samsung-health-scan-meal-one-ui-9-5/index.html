Samsung Health gets a Galaxy AI 'Scan meal' calorie estimator, possibly paid later

The claim

Samsung Health will add Scan meal, a Galaxy AI feature that identifies food, ingredients and portion sizes from a photo and estimates calories; it is labeled beta and its terms say it could become a paid feature.

Why we rated it Confirmed

SamMobile found the feature inside the Samsung Health app, including Samsung's own beta label, accuracy warnings and a note that photos are processed on servers. That is hard evidence the feature exists, so it is Likely. The One UI 9.5 timing is SamMobile's expectation, and 'could become paid' is Samsung hedging, not a price. Update Nov 18: Samsung's official One UI 9.5 beta changelog for the Galaxy S26 lists 'Scan meals and log them instantly': one photo of a meal gives nutrition details, ingredients and a calorie estimate, available in the US only. The feature is real and part of One UI 9.5, so this resolves.

Outcome

Correct on the feature: meal scanning with ingredient and calorie estimates is in Samsung's One UI 9.5 changelog. It arrived earlier than expected (in the Galaxy S26 beta on Nov 18 rather than first with the S27) and is US-only. Whether it becomes paid later is still unknown; the changelog says nothing about pricing.
