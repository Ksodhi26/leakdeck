Samsung readies 14- and 16-inch Android laptops codenamed 'Origin' with Snapdragon X Elite

The claim

Samsung is building two Wi-Fi-only Android laptops, codenamed origin14 and origin16 (model numbers XE940XSA and XE960XSA), with a 12-core Snapdragon X Elite; the 16-inch model may have a touchscreen.

Why we rated it Likely

Android Authority found the codenames, model numbers and chip profile in Samsung's own One UI 9.5 beta code, and an XE940XSA running Android 17 on a Snapdragon X Elite appeared on Geekbench in October. That is hard evidence of real devices. The branding (Googlebook or not), price and launch timing are unknown; the timing is Android Authority's guess.
