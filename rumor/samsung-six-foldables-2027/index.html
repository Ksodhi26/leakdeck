The claim

Samsung's 2027 line-up will have six Galaxy Z devices: Z Flip9, Z Fold9, Z Fold9 Pro, Z Fold9 Ultra, Z TriFold2 and a rollable Z Slide, with the TriFold 2 arriving first.

Why we rated it Rumor

From tipster @TheGalox_ on X, relayed by 9to5Google and Android Authority. Four of the names match an earlier report; the Fold9 Pro and Z Slide are new and uncorroborated. Samsung Display has shown slidable screens, but earlier reports pointed to a rollable phone in the first half of 2028, so the Slide's 2027 timing is the weakest part.
