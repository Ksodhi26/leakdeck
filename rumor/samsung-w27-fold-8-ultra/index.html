The claim

Samsung will release a W27 in China, a more premium Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra in blue with gold accents.

Why we rated it Likely

Samsung has now confirmed on Weibo a China launch on Oct 29 for a gold-framed book-style foldable, and its linked page mentions 'W2026' and the W series. That makes a W-series Fold 8 Ultra variant very likely, but Samsung has not used the W27 name or confirmed specs; Ice Universe says it matches the Z Fold 8 Ultra. We will resolve this on Oct 29.
