The claim

Bloober Team's remake of the first Silent Hill, cast under the codename 'Project Noise', is aiming for a 2027 release.

Why we rated it Rumor

Konami announced the remake in mid-2025, about eight months after Bloober's well-received Silent Hill 2 remake, but has given no date. The 2027 window and the first two cast names come from a single MP1st report, which VGC and IGN have relayed; MP1st itself says it is an internal target that can move.
