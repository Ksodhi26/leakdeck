The claim

Bethesda's newly named The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel will not ship for more than two years.

Why we rated it Likely

Bethesda revealed the Sentinel subtitle on Nov 4 and said it will be 'some time' before it shares more. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier, who called Fallout 76 and Starfield timing correctly, then said the game is 'still more than two years away' and doubled down on Reddit; Windows Central's Jez Corden said the same. Two well-sourced reporters agreeing makes this Likely, though release windows this far out often move.
