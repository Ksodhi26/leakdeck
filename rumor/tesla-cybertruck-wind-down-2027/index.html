The claim

Tesla will wind down Cybertruck production at Gigafactory Texas around the middle of 2027 to make room for more Cybercab production.

Why we rated it Confirmed

Elon Musk stated this directly on X on Nov 6, 2026, and major outlets including Electrek, TechCrunch and The Verge reported it the same day. Inventory runoff, parts support and any price changes after the wind-down were not detailed in the post.

Outcome

Confirmed as stated by Musk on Nov 6, 2026. Production wind-down targeted for around mid-2027 at Giga Texas; Cybercab cited as the reason.
