The claim

Tesla plans an autonomous version of the Model Y with no steering wheel or pedals, aimed at carrying groups of six or seven people.

Why we rated it Rumor

Elon Musk said this on stage at the New York Times DealBook Summit on Nov 18 (Electrek, InsideEVs). It comes from Tesla's CEO, but there is no timeline, price, factory or regulatory path. A second car without controls would also face the same federal safety-standard fight as the Cybercab, and Musk's product timelines have a poor track record. We rate it a Rumor until Tesla puts a product and date behind it.
