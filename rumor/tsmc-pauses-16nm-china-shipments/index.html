TSMC holds some 16nm-and-below shipments under new US export rules

The claim

TSMC has paused some shipments of 16nm/14nm-and-newer chips to Chinese (and some non-Chinese) designers while it checks compliance with a new US BIS rule that presumes such chips are controlled unless their performance is certified, which could hit phone, TV and car chips.

Why we rated it Likely

Reported by Nikkei Asia and relayed by Tom's Hardware; Reuters separately reports TSMC tightening China curbs under the new rules. The BIS rule itself is public, which makes the reaction plausible, but TSMC has not detailed which orders are held or for how long. Likely.
