The claim

The vivo X500 Ultra launches in China around March-April 2027 with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, a flat 6.85-inch BOE LTPO screen, an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor, a 7,000-8,000mAh battery, a 50MP Sony LYT910 35mm main camera and a 200MP 1/1.2-inch telephoto.

Why we rated it Rumor

All from Weibo tipster Digital Chat Station via GSMArena. On Oct 21 he said the latest engineering sample tests a 200MP 1/1.2-inch telephoto sensor, larger than the X300 Ultra's 1/1.4-inch. GSMArena's two write-ups disagree on the battery (about 7,000mAh on Oct 19, about 8,000mAh on Oct 21), and engineering samples often change. The chip choice is the expected default for a 2027 Ultra; vivo has not announced the phone. On Oct 23 he added that the engineering sample's 35mm main camera uses a 50MP Sony LYT910 sensor (down from 200MP on the X300 Ultra, though with larger pixels). Separately, a global model (V2660) and a Brazil 'Jovi' model appeared in an IMEI database, which supports a launch outside China but says nothing about specs.
