The claim

The vivo X500 Ultra launches in China around March-April 2027 with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, a flat 6.85-inch 2K 144Hz BOE LTPO screen, an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor and a battery of about 7,000mAh.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Weibo tipster Digital Chat Station via GSMArena. The chip choice is the expected default for a 2027 Ultra flagship; the screen and battery details are unverified, and vivo has not announced the phone.
