The claim

Microsoft's Project Helix console was conceived as a console-PC hybrid with full Windows PC games, stores such as Steam, and no subscription needed for online multiplayer, though new Xbox leadership and rising costs may change that.

Why we rated it Rumor

From Windows Central's Jez Corden, citing sources, relayed by GameSpot and GamesRadar. It describes an original plan made under Phil Spencer and Sarah Bond, and Corden himself says it may not survive under the new leadership, so it says little about the final product. Microsoft has not commented. Rumor.
