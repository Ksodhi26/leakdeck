The vanilla Xiaomi 18 launches in December with 100W charging

Rating
Likely
Status
Active
Expected
December 2026 (China, reported)
First reported

Last updated
Nov 20, 2026
Categories
Google / Android

The claim

A standard Xiaomi 18 is still coming this year, around December in China, with 100W wired charging, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6, a 200MP main camera and a 7,200mAh battery.

Why we rated it Likely

A new 3C certification (via GSMArena) lists a Xiaomi phone with 100W charging that is believed, not confirmed, to be the Xiaomi 18. The December timing and other specs come from earlier leaks. Certification is hard evidence that a device exists, but its identity is inferred and Xiaomi has not mentioned the base model. Update Nov 12: a dealer poster on Weibo shows offline retailers in China taking refundable blind deposits for the standard Xiaomi 18 (GSMArena, Gizmochina), and Digital Chat Station tips a 2nm mid-size model with up to 16GB/1TB. Dealer pre-orders plus the 3C listing are hard signs of a near launch, so this moves to Likely; Xiaomi itself has not confirmed a date or specs. Update Nov 17: XiaomiTime reports a Xiaomi Weibo poster announcing blind bookings for the 'Xiaomi 18 Standard Edition', and Lei Jun was photographed with a white phone believed to be the Xiaomi 18 at the APEC CEO Summit (Gizmochina). The phone is now effectively official, but Xiaomi has not given a date or confirmed the 100W and battery specs, so it stays Likely. Update Nov 18: tipster Debayan Roy (Gadgetsdata) posted a purported live image of a white Xiaomi 18 with a metal frame, a three-camera square island and no rear screen, and lists 100W wired charging, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 and a 200MP main camera, but a battery of about 7,000mAh (not 7,200mAh) and a 50MP telephoto instead of the 200MP periscope from earlier leaks (GSMArena, Gadgets360). The 100W and chip details agree; the exact battery size is now disputed. Stays Likely. Update Nov 20: Xiaomi's own online store in China now takes blind pre-reservations for the Xiaomi 18, still without a date, design or specs (GSMArena). That makes a near launch even more certain, but the 100W and battery details remain leaks, so it stays Likely.
