The claim

Xiaomi is polishing a big-screen 'Xiaomi 18 Max' with Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 and a battery above 8,000mAh, succeeding the 17 Max.

Why we rated it Shaky

Digital Chat Station only said an unnamed brand is polishing a big-screen Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 phone; Chinese outlets (ITHome, MyDrivers) and fan site XiaomiHive linked it to a Xiaomi 18 Max. The brand link and the May timing are guesses based on the 17 Max, and Xiaomi has said nothing. Shaky.
