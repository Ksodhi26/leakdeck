Xiaomi 18 Pro and Pro Max cost EUR 1,449-1,849 in Europe with smaller batteries

The claim

The European Xiaomi 18 Pro will cost EUR 1,449 (256GB) or EUR 1,649 (512GB) with a 6,000mAh-rated battery and 90W charging, and the 18 Pro Max EUR 1,849 (512GB) with a 7,050mAh-rated battery and 100W, both smaller than the Chinese versions' 7,000mAh and 8,500mAh.

Why we rated it Rumor

Prices come from a single unnamed report relayed by GSMArena. The battery figures are more solid: Xiaomi's EU energy labels are already published, which also means a European launch is close. Xiaomi has not confirmed prices or a date.
