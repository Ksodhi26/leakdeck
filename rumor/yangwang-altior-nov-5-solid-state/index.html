The claim

BYD will unveil its 5.5-meter Yangwang Altior executive sedan in Dubai around Nov 5, as the first car with BYD's solid-state batteries, priced around 2 million yuan.

Why we rated it Rumor

The name is official: on Oct 27 BYD revealed the sedan as the Yangwang Altior (DingYao in China) with a teaser, and Yangwang's Hu Xiaoqing had already said the global launch will be in the UAE. The Nov 5 date comes from CarNewsChina citing people familiar with the project; BYD has not announced a date. The solid-state battery and the roughly 2 million yuan price are reports only, and BYD's Stella Li has said its first solid-state car debuts in 2027, so that part is the weakest. Rear-hinged coach doors are visible in teasers and prototype photos. Update Oct 30: Hu Xiaoqing confirmed the Altior has rear-hinged (suicide) doors, a first for BYD, and previewed a dot-matrix grille; a teaser shows it at a BYD Flash Charging station. BYD still has not confirmed the Nov 5 date or a solid-state pack.
