Rumor tracker

Leaks are not all equal. We rate each rumor by the quality of the evidence and keep the sources next to it. Ratings change as news comes in, and resolved rumors stay up as a track record.

Confirmed
Announced by the company or shown in an official source.
Likely
Multiple credible outlets or hard evidence (certifications, databases, supply chain), but no official word.
Rumor
One or few sources, an estimate, or a leaker claim. Plausible but unverified.
Shaky
Weak sourcing, conflicting reports, a bad track record, or a guess presented as news.
Debunked
Contradicted by the company or by clear evidence.

DebunkedUpdated Oct 17, 2026

NVIDIA has stopped making the RTX 5090

Claim: NVIDIA has halted production of the GeForce RTX 5090 and is redirecting the GB202 chip to its professional RTX Pro cards.

Real signs pointed that way: Micro Center reclassified the card 'Not Carried', many stores sold out, and NVIDIA pulled it from its own marketplace. But on Oct 16 NVIDIA's head of personal computing, Jeff Fisher, told reporters in Taipei the RTX 5090 is not end-of-life and NVIDIA will keep shipping it, while admitting supply is tight. By Oct 17 the card was listed again on NVIDIA's US store, though not in stock.

Outcome: Debunked by NVIDIA: the RTX 5090 has not been discontinued, though it remains scarce and expensive.
