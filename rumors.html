Rumor tracker

Leaks are not all equal. We rate each rumor by the quality of the evidence and keep the sources next to it. Ratings change as news comes in, and resolved rumors stay up as a track record.

Confirmed
Announced by the company or shown in an official source.
Likely
Multiple credible outlets or hard evidence (certifications, databases, supply chain), but no official word.
Rumor
One or few sources, an estimate, or a leaker claim. Plausible but unverified.
Shaky
Weak sourcing, conflicting reports, a bad track record, or a guess presented as news.
Debunked
Contradicted by the company or by clear evidence.
